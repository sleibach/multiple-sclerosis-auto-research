## Explicit Non-Triggers

The following do not count as contradictions:

- broad disease-mechanism reviews without signal-specific direction or cohort
  overlap;
- drug labels or mechanism summaries without paired response data;
- gene-disease association listings without effect direction;
- single paper claims that use different phenotype definitions, populations, or
  layers without a route to map them to the project finding;
- model or RPT suggestions without a rerunnable grounding route.
