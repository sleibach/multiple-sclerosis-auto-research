# V53 CD44/CXCR4 MS Microglia Prior-Art Boundary

Status: `external-unverifiable`; `NOT_PROJECT_GROUNDED`; source: the four
source-specific records and publications cited below. This document is
segregated literature context, not evidence for a project conclusion.

## Question

Does the external literature make the provisional V53 broad-state-bounded
CD44/CXCR4 association biologically or therapeutically novel?

## Boundary Verdict

- `external-unverifiable`; `NOT_PROJECT_GROUNDED`; source: the four records
  above. **Biological novelty is low** for CD44 or CXCR4 as markers of activated
  or lesion-associated human MS myeloid states.
- `external-unverifiable`; `NOT_PROJECT_GROUNDED`; source: [van der Poel et al.
  2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30867424/) plus the independent lesion
  studies above. **Analytical novelty is narrower and moderate at most**: the
  exact patient-equal disjoint CD44+CXCR4 score, age/region sensitivity, and
  broad-state attenuation are a project reanalysis, but they use an existing
  public cohort and are not independently replicated.
- `external-unverifiable`; `NOT_PROJECT_GROUNDED`; source: [Moll et al.
  2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19139298/) and [Hsiao et al.
  2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34054860/). **Therapeutic novelty is not
  established**: existing CXCR4 biology does not supply an MS-beneficial
  intervention direction, cell-state selectivity, or evidence that altering
  the score improves disease.

The project-grounded status is unchanged: a robust within-GSE111972,
broad-state-bounded association that requires an independent cohort under the
frozen V53 replication specification. External agreement provides context but
does not upgrade the evidence grade.
