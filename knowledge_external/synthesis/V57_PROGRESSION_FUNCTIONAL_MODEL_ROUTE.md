# V57 Progression Functional-Model Route

Status: mixed-class method synthesis; `external-unverifiable` items are `NOT_PROJECT_GROUNDED` context, not project evidence (source: `docs/knowledge/EPISTEMIC_CLASSES.md`).

## Why This Method Is New To The Project

- **[grounded; source: `docs/history/PROGRESSION_FRONTIER_V54.md`]** Held public data did not establish a portable progression state or a direction-resolved intervention route.
- **[grounded-method; source: `analysis/v57_measurement_invariance/REPORT.md`]** Shared gene names did not establish invariant module architecture across the tested environments.
- **[external-unverifiable; source: `knowledge_external/records/method_ms_glia_enriched_organoid_2025.json`]** A published human glia-enriched organoid offers microglial, oligodendrocyte, myelin, imaging, and single-cell readouts in one platform, but its source-reported biology is not project evidence.
- **[external-unverifiable; source: `knowledge_external/records/method_microglia_oligodendrocyte_assembloid_2024.json`]** A separate human assembloid provides a technical precedent for testing whether a microglial perturbation changes oligodendrocyte-lineage and myelination-related outcomes.

This route asks a causal functional question that another observational module
scan cannot answer: does changing a microglial state improve or harm neural-
glial support in an independent human context?

## Stage-Gated Experiment

### Stage 0: Platform and variance pilot

1. Cross at least two microglial donor backgrounds with at least two neural-
   organoid donor backgrounds; do not confound cell lineage with donor.
2. Randomize organoids to conditions within production batch and blind image,
   viability, and molecular analysis.
3. Use donor-pair as the inferential unit. Organoids, fields, cells, and wells
   are technical or nested replicates, never independent biological samples.
4. Estimate variance while treatment identities remain blinded, then apply the
   verified synthetic assurance router in
   `analysis/v57_blinded_variance_expanded_grid/REPORT.md`. Real variance, not
   synthetic assumptions, sets the confirmation panel.
5. Stop if cell composition, compact-myelin quality, microglial persistence, or
   batch reproducibility fails a frozen QC criterion.

### Stage 1: Direction-resolved calibration

Use CRISPRi and CRISPRa with multiple guides for only a small, pre-audited
candidate set. Include non-targeting controls, positive assay controls, and
toxicity controls. The fixed question is direction-resolved rescue, not a
favorite target.

### Stage 2: Orthogonal functional confirmation

Require all of the following in fresh donor pairs:

- improvement without reversal in prespecified microglial-state readouts;
- no loss of viability in either donor context;
- improved or preserved oligodendrocyte maturation and MBP-positive myelin
  support;
- no worsening of axonal/neuronal integrity;
- guide and direction coherence;
- replication in a simpler 2D/co-culture assay so an organoid-specific artifact
  is not mistaken for transfer.

The multi-outcome, context-safe confirmation rule remains compensatory-proof:
one large transcript effect cannot cancel toxicity or impaired neural support.

## Why Not Start With A Large Organoid Screen

- **[external-unverifiable; source: `knowledge_external/records/method_ms_glia_enriched_organoid_2025.json`]** The source model reports limited iPSC-line count, imperfect compact myelin/cytoarchitecture, and microglial persistence measured in weeks.
- **[grounded-method; source: `analysis/v57_perturbation_design/REPORT.md`]** Optimal/compressed pair selection loses rank advantage when interaction descriptors do not generalize.

Therefore organoids are a confirmation layer after a cheaper direction screen,
not a discovery-scale fishing platform. Large spatial or combinatorial
expansion occurs only after a replicated human-cell perturbation clears the
functional gate.

## Kill Criteria

Reject or redesign the route if any holds:

1. effects do not replicate across crossed donor pairs;
2. a molecular rescue lacks functional myelin/neural support;
3. benefit is explained by cell-composition, viability, or production batch;
4. CRISPRi/a directions or guides disagree;
5. the simpler co-culture and organoid results reverse;
6. blinded pilot variance requires resources beyond the predeclared grid.

## Decision

This is the strongest newly identified progression-oriented **method route**,
not a therapeutic finding. It joins a direction-resolving human microglial
screen to a multicellular human functional assay and makes myelin/neural
support, donor transfer, and safety first-class gates. No candidate is promoted
until real experiments pass them.
