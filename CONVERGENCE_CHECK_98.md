# CONVERGENCE_CHECK_98 - TAGAP Benchmark

Timestamp: 2026-05-28 08:23 CEST.

## Track Beliefs

- Genetics-first track: `TAGAP` is one of the strongest outside-catalog broad
  autoimmune genetics candidates.
- Local T-cell state track: raw h5ad T-cell compartments do not show positive
  recurrent disease-state TAGAP or TAGAP/RhoGTPase activation signal.
- Translational track: no reachable TAGAP-directed non-broad-suppression
  modality is available in local evidence.

## Agreement

`TAGAP` should not be promoted. It is useful as a benchmark demonstrating that
MS plus pan-autoimmune genetic anchoring is insufficient without cell-state,
direction, perturbation, and intervention gates.

## Disagreement

The unresolved question is whether another lymphoid-niche genetically anchored
axis, especially `TNFSF14`/LIGHT-LTBR, deserves a specific forcing test. It
appears in MS genetics and in the Wave146 TLS module, but has not been audited
as a target-level route in this post-Wave145 branch.

## Decision

Run a TNFSF14/LIGHT-LTBR lymphoid-niche audit next unless existing local
artifacts show a direct closure.
