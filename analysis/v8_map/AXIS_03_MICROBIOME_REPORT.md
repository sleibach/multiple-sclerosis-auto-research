# V8 Microbiome Axis

Interpretation: on the microbiome axis only, MS is placed nearer to IBD and T1D than to RA when the criterion is longitudinal gut microbial-immune/metabolite evidence. This is not a binary disease clustering. RA is intermediate because its gut-joint microbiome route is plausible but mechanistically different, with Prevotella/oral-pathogen themes rather than the MS/IBD SCFA-barrier/mucin axis.

| disease | placement | grade | confidence | main caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crohn disease | near | provisional | medium | Near on gut microbial-immune axis, not proof that Crohn and MS share initiating causes. |
| ulcerative colitis | near | provisional | medium | Microbiome proximity is stronger for gut-barrier biology than for CNS lesion biology. |
| rheumatoid arthritis | intermediate | provisional | low | RA microbiome evidence may be mechanistically real but points to gut-joint/Prevotella/oral pathogen routes rather than the MS gut-brain pattern. |
| type 1 diabetes mellitus | near | provisional | medium | Near on longitudinal microbiome/SCFA/early-autoimmunity axis, not on target tissue or age-of-onset biology. |
| psoriasis | intermediate | provisional | low | Skin-gut axis is plausible, but longitudinal/causal microbial evidence remains thinner than IBD/T1D. |
| Sjogren syndrome | intermediate | provisional | low | Evidence supports dysbiosis but not a well-resolved MS-shared microbial mechanism. |
| celiac disease | near | provisional | low | Near is driven by gut-barrier longitudinal design, not by shared antigen trigger with MS. |
| systemic lupus erythematosus | intermediate | provisional | low | SLE may be closer to MS on infectious-trigger/IFN axes than on microbiome axis. |
| Hashimoto thyroiditis | unresolved | provisional | low | AITD is not absent; it is unresolved pending thyroid-focused microbiome sources. |
| Graves disease | unresolved | provisional | low | AITD is not absent; it is unresolved pending thyroid-focused microbiome sources. |
