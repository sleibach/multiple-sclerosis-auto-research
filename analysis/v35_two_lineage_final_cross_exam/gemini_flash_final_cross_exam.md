1. **Lysosomal APC bottleneck**: High mechanistic potential; links IFN-driven metabolic stress to antigen presentation.
    *   **Fatal Weakness**: Lacks spatial/functional validation of lysosomal flux in patient-derived APCs.
    *   **Decisive Test**: Pulse-chase antigen processing assay in patient-derived iPSC-monocytes under IFN-stimulation.

2. **T/B compartment remodeling gate**: Strong clinical correlation; suggests a druggable checkpoint for immune tolerance.
    *   **Fatal Weakness**: Correlation may be a downstream effect of systemic inflammation rather than a driver.
    *   **Decisive Test**: Longitudinal single-cell TCR/BCR repertoire tracking in a tofacitinib-naive vs. treated cohort.

3. **Postpartum HLA-II/CD64 APC-arm imbalance**: High clinical utility; addresses the "pregnancy-relapse" paradox.
    *   **Fatal Weakness**: Confounded by massive hormonal shifts masking the specific APC-driven signal.
    *   **Decisive Test**: Longitudinal mass cytometry (CyTOF) of postpartum peripheral blood vs. CSF in MS patients.

4. **Metabolic/sterol setpoint**: Addresses fundamental cellular homeostasis; potentially universal across autoimmune phenotypes.
    *   **Fatal Weakness**: High noise-to-signal ratio; difficult to distinguish from general metabolic dysregulation.
    *   **Decisive Test**: CRISPR-Cas9 screen in APCs targeting top-hit sterol genes to observe MHC-II surface expression.

5. **MS-SLE EBV imprint**: High disease-relevance; leverages known viral triggers.
    *   **Fatal Weakness**: EBV specificity is statistically indistinguishable from background noise.
    *   **Decisive Test**: Single-cell BCR sequencing of EBV-specific B-cells to identify cross-reactive autoantigen binding.

6. **Complement/lipid progressive axis**: Addresses the most difficult clinical phenotype (progression).
    *   **Fatal Weakness**: Donor-aware lesion testing suggests the signal is secondary to tissue damage.
    *   **Decisive Test**: Spatial transcriptomics on early-stage MS lesions to determine if lipid-complement colocalization precedes demyelination.
