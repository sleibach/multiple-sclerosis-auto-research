# HYP_V6_005 - OPC CD74 Lesion-Stress State

Status: alive  
Tier: Tier -1  
Opened: 2026-05-28 20:51 CEST

## Hypothesis

Nominal residual `CD74` signal in OPC/oligodendroglial lesion compartments may
reflect a noncanonical stress, antigen-presentation-like, or remyelination-
failure state distinct from immune-cell MIF/CD74 targeting.

## Opening Evidence

In MS pseudobulk component testing, the expected immune-cell residual CD74
signal failed, but nominal positive residual CD74 appeared in OPC contrasts:

- inactive-edge versus control residual delta `0.823574133560422`, Hedges g
  `2.4026383281291372`, p `0.013912513736498135`;
- lesion-core versus control residual delta `0.8760042865525401`, Hedges g
  `2.615580201377954`, p `0.023540505154801952`;
- residual FDR was not significant (`0.5043699662983264`).

## Tier -1 Interpretation

This does not rescue MIF/CD74 as an immune target. It opens a separate
oligodendroglial stress-state hypothesis requiring cell-resolved validation.

## First Independent Checks

- Query single-nucleus MS lesion atlases for OPC/oligodendrocyte CD74/HLA-II
  expression and coexpression with stress/remyelination markers.
- Test whether OPC CD74 is associated with lesion edge versus core, not only
  disease versus control.
