# HYP_V6_009 - TREM2 Route-Split Repair Versus Activation

Status: alive  
Tier: Tier -1  
Opened: 2026-05-28 20:51 CEST

## Hypothesis

TREM2 may split into two mechanistic routes in autoimmune tissue: a repair or
efferocytic route and a lipid-loaded chronic activation route. Direct agonism or
antagonism is too coarse unless route and disease stage are specified.

## Opening Evidence

V5 demoted TREM2 because MS-specific route-split repair-versus-activation
evidence was absent. Under Tier -1, that absence specifies the next hypothesis:
the biology may depend on resolving which TREM2 state is protective.

## First Independent Checks

- Search lesion and autoimmune tissue atlases for TREM2 coexpression with
  repair/efferocytosis versus inflammatory/lipid-loaded modules.
- Test whether TREM2-high states separate by disease stage, lesion rim, or
  treatment response.
