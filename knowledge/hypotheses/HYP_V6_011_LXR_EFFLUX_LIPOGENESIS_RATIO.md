Status: alive  
Tier: Tier -1  
Opened: 2026-05-28 20:51 CEST

## Hypothesis

The actionable LXR/ABCA1/ABCG1 biology is not global LXR agonism, but selective
promotion of cholesterol efflux without inducing lipogenesis or broad
immunometabolic liabilities.

## Opening Evidence

The direct LXR target claim was demoted for direction, causal, and selectivity
gaps. That failure mode specifies a Tier -1 ratio hypothesis: efflux output
relative to SREBF/lipogenesis output may be the relevant state variable.

## First Independent Checks

- Compute ABCA1/ABCG1 efflux versus SREBF/FASN/SCD lipogenesis ratios in local
  lipid-lysosomal module outputs.
- Search perturbation data for compounds that separate efflux from lipogenesis.
