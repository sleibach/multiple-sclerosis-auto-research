# HYP_V6_003 - Postpartum T-Cell Trafficking Readiness

Status: alive  
Tier: Tier -1  
Opened: 2026-05-28 20:51 CEST

## Hypothesis

MS postpartum relapse biology may be driven less by late-pregnancy T-cell
activation and more by postpartum restoration of T-cell trafficking programs.

## Opening Evidence

`E-MTAB-12260` sorted MS T-cell RNA-seq did not reproduce late-pregnancy
IFN/APC or MIF/CD74 activation, but did show postpartum trafficking Th increase
versus third trimester:

- delta `0.3020256988998088`;
- Hedges g `0.5685553671142366`;
- Welch p `0.03795138383060487`.

## Tier -1 Interpretation

This shifts the pregnancy axis from activation-state to access/trafficking
state. It also provides a potential bridge to postpartum relapse prevention
without relying on broad immunosuppression during pregnancy.

## First Independent Checks

- Test T-cell trafficking modules in RA/SLE pregnancy timecourses.
- Search for postpartum MS relapse cohorts with T-cell or serum chemokine data.
- Compare module genes against approved MS trafficking interventions as a
  mechanistic plausibility check, not as a therapeutic claim.
