Status: alive

## Hypothesis

LTA4H may require lipidomic rather than transcriptomic stratification. The
surviving hypothesis is a leukotriene-biased subgroup within lipid-lysosomal
myeloid disease, not a generic LTA4H expression target.

## Opening Evidence

V5 demoted LTA4H because transcriptomic and cross-disease evidence did not
support Tier 0. The failure mode is informative: LTA4H biology should be tested
where leukotriene pathway metabolites are measured.

## First Independent Checks

  subgroup structure.
