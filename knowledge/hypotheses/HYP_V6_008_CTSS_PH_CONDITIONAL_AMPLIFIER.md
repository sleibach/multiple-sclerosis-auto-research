# HYP_V6_008 - CTSS pH-Conditional Lysosomal Amplifier

Status: alive  
Tier: Tier -1  
Opened: 2026-05-28 20:51 CEST

## Hypothesis

CTSS may not be an upstream autoimmune target, but a lysosomal-pH-dependent
amplifier within antigen-processing-high APC states. A selective or conditional
CTSS intervention would need to act only in the high-activity lysosomal state,
not as broad CTSS blockade.

## Opening Evidence

V5 demotion held for direct CTSS therapeutic targeting because available local
evidence did not show CTSS-specific causal or predictive value beyond APC,
HLA-II, IFI30, and IFN programs. The failure mode nevertheless points to a
narrower conditional-amplifier hypothesis.

## First Independent Checks

- Mine datasets for CTSS residuals after HLA-II/CD74/IFI30/IFN adjustment.
- Look for perturbations or chemistry that alter CTSS lysosomal readouts without
  suppressing global APC state.
