# MerTK / TAM Family

Status: demoted  
V4 tier: Tier 0  
Last updated: 2026-05-28

## V3 History

V3 considered efferocytosis/resolution biology repeatedly but direct MerTK/TAM
routes were not matured to a V3 finding.

## V4 Recalibration Question

Can MerTK/TAM modulation be specified by ligand, receptor, cell type, disease
stage, or combination rather than generic efferocytosis enhancement?

## Current V4 Contribution

None as an active direct therapeutic target nomination.

MerTK/TAM remains useful as:
- an efferocytosis/resolution comparator axis,
- a possible remission/resolution biomarker axis,
- and a candidate perturbation/natural-experiment route if correct-direction
  TAM restoration can be shown to drive resolution rather than marker-only
  macrophage abundance, fibrosis, or tumor-like AXL/GAS6 programs.

The demotion is not because TAM biology is crowded. Locally cached evidence did
not show equivalent correct-direction autoimmune MERTK/TAM agonism failed
clinically with target engagement. The demotion holds because V3 did not produce
the causal, perturbational, direction-resolved, or modality evidence needed to
nominate MERTK/TAM as a target.

## V4 Recalibration Verdict

Verdict 3: evidence-driven demotion holds.

## Next Tier 0 Test

Do not reopen generic MERTK/TAM expression or generic efferocytosis enhancement.

Allowed future re-entry test:
- Run a correct-direction TAM restoration screen.
- In MS lesion/myeloid data, require a TAM restoration signature associated
  with repair/efferocytosis rather than lipid-loaded chronic lesion state.
- In at least one RA or lupus dataset, require high or induced
  `MERTK/GAS6/PROS1` to mark remission/resolution rather than fibrosis or
  immunosuppressive persistence.
- In perturbation data, require MERTK activation or ligand restoration to
  increase efferocytosis and reduce inflammatory APC/IFN-HLA-II output without
  increasing fibrosis/tumor-like AXL/GAS6 programs.
- Require evidence stronger than generic macrophage abundance or generic
  efferocytosis markers.

Pass threshold: at least one disease-relevant perturbation or natural-experiment
dataset plus one independent autoimmune replication.
