## V3 History

V3 Wave169/Wave170 rescued `XMD-1150/LRRK2` from L1000 and external ChEMBL
target-quality evidence. Wave171 demoted the generic claim because LRRK2
inhibition has EAE/neuroinflammation literature and patent scope naming MS and
autoimmune indications.

## V4 Recalibration Question

Does any non-generic LRRK2 contribution remain: biomarker-defined myeloid
subgroup, combination, different direction, or disease-stage-specific use?

## Current V4 Contribution

Verdict: **Demotion was prior-art-driven for the generic claim, but V4
contribution remains possible only as a new subgroup, combination, or
biomarker-defined mechanism.**

Generic LRRK2 inhibition for MS is not a V4 contribution.

## Recalibration Under V4 Prior-Art Rule

V3 Wave171 was too binary if interpreted as "LRRK2 is known, therefore dead."
Under V4, the correct reading is narrower:

- Generic LRRK2 inhibition for MS/neuroinflammation is crowded and not novel.
- Directly equivalent clinical failure in MS was not established in V3.
- Therefore the target is not biologically invalidated, but the generic claim
  is not enough for V4.

Prior-art grade: `P1 high crowding`.

Key prior-art context:

- `phases/v3/results/wave171_lrrk2_prior_art_killtest/REPORT.md` documents the V3
  kill test.
- Google Patents `WO2024182689A1` explicitly discusses LRRK2 inhibitors and
  includes immune-system diseases such as multiple sclerosis, rheumatoid
  arthritis, SLE, type 1 diabetes, and Sjogren's syndrome in the indication
  scope: <https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2024182689A1/en>.
- ClinicalTrials.gov `NCT05348785` establishes CNS-penetrant LRRK2 inhibitor
  clinical development in Parkinson's disease, not MS:
  <https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05348785>.

V3 evidence retained:

- Wave169 L1000 deconvolution ranked `XMD-1150/LRRK2` as the top repurposing
  candidate.
- Wave170 external ChEMBL target-quality audit supported LRRK2 target quality:
  `CHEMBL1075104`, single protein, `1000` downloaded activity records, `312`
  unique molecules, best downloaded nM `0.39`.
- Wave96/V3 local state evidence was IBD-skewed and did not provide a strong
  MS-specific target anchor.

## V4 Contribution Still Possible

A valid LRRK2 V4 claim would need at least one of:

- biomarker-defined myeloid/APC subgroup,
- combination with remyelination or resolution therapy,
- disease-stage-specific progressive MS mechanism,
- non-equivalent modality or biased pathway modulation,
- longitudinal or natural-experiment evidence connecting LRRK2 state to
  disease transition.

Without one of these, LRRK2 remains a comparator rather than an active V4
program.

## Next Tier 0 Test

Search treatment-resistance and longitudinal myeloid-state dimensions for
LRRK2-specific predictors. Do not reopen generic LRRK2 inhibition.

Demote fully if no subgroup/combination/natural-experiment evidence is found in
Tier 0.
