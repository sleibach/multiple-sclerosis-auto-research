# PTGER4

Status: closed as not-a-clean-transfer-target  
Last updated: 2026-06-06  
Primary evidence file: `../../GENETICS_EQTL_WORKUP_V16.md`

## Current Verdict

`PTGER4` remains biologically important and druggable in principle, but the
MS-UC chr5 locus is signal-conflicted. It is not a simple therapeutic transfer
target for MS.

## Evidence

- V15/V16 decomposed chr5 into at least two signal classes:
  - shared component around `rs350054`;
  - distinct component around `rs62356511` / `rs1445002`.
- eQTLGen direction differs by component, creating opposite MS/UC implications.

## Limits

- Global PTGER4 agonism or antagonism cannot be inferred from the current
  genetics.
- Signal-specific cell-type QTL data would be required before reopening.

## Next Required Test

Do not spend further priority time unless signal-specific cell-type QTL or
perturbation data appears.
