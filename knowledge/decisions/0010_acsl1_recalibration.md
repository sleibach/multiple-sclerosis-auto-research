# Decision 0010: ACSL1 Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

`ACSL1` remains demoted as a therapeutic target nomination. It is retained as a
foamy/MIMS2-like MS lesion marker and wet-lab perturbation hypothesis only.

## V4 Verdict

Verdict 3: evidence-driven and modality-driven demotion holds.

## Rationale

ACSL1 was not mainly killed by binary prior-art gating. The original hypothesis
had novelty, but heavier scrutiny weakened the causal and translational claim:
ACSL1 lost incremental value beyond the broader lipid/lysosomal myeloid module,
cross-autoimmune recurrence was inconsistent, ABM simulation worsened lesion
dynamics under ACSL1 reduction, and no CNS/microglia-engaged clinical modality
exists.

## Consequence

Do not reopen ACSL1 for computational Tier 0 target work. Reopen only if new
human perturbation/rescue or larger spatial/protein data demonstrate causality,
incremental value beyond the lipid/lysosomal module, and repair-safe CNS or
microglia-selective target engagement.
