# Decision 0002 - LRRK2 Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

LRRK2 is parked, not globally demoted.

## Verdict

V3 correctly killed the generic `LRRK2 inhibition for MS` claim, but V4 does
not treat prior art as target-invalidating. LRRK2 may re-enter only if a new V4
contribution is specified: subgroup, combination, modality, disease-stage, or
natural-experiment-supported mechanism.

## Evidence

- V3 Wave169/Wave170: `XMD-1150/LRRK2` passed local L1000 plus external ChEMBL
  target-quality correction.
- V3 Wave171: generic LRRK2/MS claim failed novelty due to neuroinflammation
  literature and patent scope.
- Patent context: `WO2024182689A1` includes LRRK2 inhibitor claims and immune
  disease scope naming MS and related autoimmune diseases.

## Next Action

Keep `LRRK2.md` parked. Reopen only if treatment-resistance, longitudinal, or
natural-experiment dimensions identify a subgroup-specific LRRK2 mechanism.
