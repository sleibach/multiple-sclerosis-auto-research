# Decision 0006: CTSS Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

`CTSS` remains demoted as a direct therapeutic target. It is retained only as a
mechanistic comparator, pharmacodynamic readout, or possible stratification
feature within the broader antigen-processing-high APC state.

## V4 Verdict

Verdict 3: evidence-driven demotion holds.

## Rationale

V4 no longer kills candidates merely for being known targets. CTSS fails a
stronger standard: the same target/direction class has been clinically tested in
autoimmune disease, with target engagement but no convincing clinical efficacy,
and V3 mechanistic modeling placed CTSS downstream of the central IFN/HLA-II/APC
state rather than upstream of the disease transition.

Key local evidence:
- `subagents_v3/wave11_genetics_prior_art_scout_report.md`
- `subagents_v3/wave13_genetics_prior_art_reopen.md`
- `results_v3/wave16_ctsh_chembl_feasibility/cathepsin_activity_summary.tsv`
- `results_v3/wave46_central_axis_closure_audit/`
- `docs/lab_notebooks/LAB_NOTEBOOK_V3.md`

External verified evidence:
- RO5459072/petesicatib primary Sjogren phase IIa: `NCT02701985`, no clinically
  relevant ESSDAI improvement despite target engagement.
- RO5459072 celiac gluten challenge: PMID `39739628`, DOI
  `10.1111/cts.13901`, primary endpoint not met.

## Consequence

No Tier 0 budget should be spent on generic systemic CTSS inhibition. Reopen
only with a distinct V4 contribution: biomarker-defined subgroup, local delivery
with tissue-compartment rationale, or perturbation/longitudinal evidence that
CTSS is causal beyond the broader antigen-processing module.
