# Decision 0015: CHI3L1 Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

`CHI3L1` is parked as a V4 biomarker/stratification candidate and remains
demoted as a direct therapeutic target.

## V4 Verdict

Verdict 2 for biomarker use only.

## Rationale

CHI3L1/YKL-40 has strong local MS and cross-disease marker evidence, but V3 did
not support it as a causal, selectively actionable intervention point. V4 keeps
it because longitudinal/prognostic dimensions are now first-class evidence, and
CHI3L1 has a plausible biomarker contribution if it predicts trajectory or
treatment response independently of generic inflammation and tissue injury.

## Consequence

Run only a longitudinal/prognostic biomarker Tier 0 screen. Do not spend target
validation budget on direct CHI3L1 intervention unless independent causality and
selective-action evidence emerge.
