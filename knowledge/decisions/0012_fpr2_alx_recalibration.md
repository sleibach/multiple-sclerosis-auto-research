Date: 2026-05-28 12:39 CEST

## Decision

`FPR2_ALX` is parked at Tier 0 under Verdict 2.

The V4 contribution is biased FPR2/ALX pro-resolution agonism or ANXA1-mimetic
biology in a cell-state-specific resolution/efferocytosis context, likely
IBD/lupus-nephritis-first. Generic FPR2 agonism and generic MS pro-resolution
claims remain closed.

## Rationale

Prior art is high but not P0 target-invalidating: the local record did not show
an equivalent biased FPR2/ALX autoimmune or MS intervention that failed
clinically for target-mechanistic reasons. However, V3 blockers were also
evidence-driven: weak/negative MS white-matter support, no target-resolved
genetics, unresolved direct efferocytosis perturbation, and ligand-bias sign
risk.

## Next Action

in disease-relevant macrophage/microglia systems. Do not spend Tier 1 budget on
expression-only or bulk signature analyses.
