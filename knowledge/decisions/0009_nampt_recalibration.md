# Decision 0009: NAMPT Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

`NAMPT` re-enters V4 as alive Tier 0 only for a constrained eNAMPT or
biomarker-defined transient-modulation branch. Generic systemic intracellular
NAMPT catalytic inhibition remains closed.

## V4 Verdict

Verdict 2: demotion was partly prior-art-driven, but a constrained V4
contribution exists.

## Rationale

V3 mixed heavy prior art, systemic NAD-depletion safety, and weak causal
direction. Under V4, heavy prior art is not a gate unless equivalent clinical
failure invalidates the target/direction. No local evidence showed such a
failure for NAMPT in autoimmune disease. However, V3 data also do not support
generic NAMPT inhibition: the strongest recurrence is IBD/T1D metabolic-state
signal, with weak MS and genetic anchoring.

## Consequence

Tier 0 must split extracellular/inflammatory eNAMPT biology from intracellular
NAD-depletion biology. Advance only if NAMPT signal is MS lesion/CSF-EV or
myeloid-state enriched, survives `HIF1A`/glycolysis/IFN/NF-kB/myeloid-density
adjustment, replicates outside IBD, and has a non-NAD-depleting or tightly
bounded modality.
