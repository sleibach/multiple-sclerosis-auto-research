# Decision 0007: TREM2 Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

`TREM2` remains demoted as an active therapeutic target nomination. It is
retained as a repair-biology comparator and possible stratification/readout
axis.

## V4 Verdict

Verdict 3: evidence-driven demotion holds.

## Rationale

The AL002/TREM2 agonist prior art is not target-invalidating for MS under V4,
because it is not an equivalent progressive-MS/remyelination-enriched trial.
The demotion holds for non-prior-art reasons: no target-resolved causal support,
weak/null cross-disease gene signal, missing perturbation/foundation alignment,
and unresolved direction between repair, chronic activation, shedding, and
biomarker-only TREM2 abundance.

## Consequence

Do not reopen generic TREM2 agonism. Reopen only after a route-split cuprizone
perturbation audit (`GSE302857`, `GSE66926`, `GSE70475`) shows consistent
repair/lipid-clearance effects separable from generic activation/stress in at
least two datasets.
