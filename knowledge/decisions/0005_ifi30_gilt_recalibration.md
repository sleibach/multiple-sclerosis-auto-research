# Decision 0005 - IFI30/GILT Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

Direct IFI30/GILT intervention is demoted. IFI30/GILT remains parked only as a
biomarker or pathway readout for safer upstream modulation.

## Verdict

Verdict 3: evidence-driven demotion holds.

## Evidence

IFI30 has real genetics/QTL and APC-state biology, but direct intervention
fails:

- weak/null MS white-matter expression anchor,
- no direct perturbation/model support,
- no reachable drug modality,
- antigen-processing/host-defense risk.

## Next Action

Do not pursue direct IFI30/GILT. Test IFI30 as a treatment-response biomarker
only if suitable datasets are available.
