# Decision 0011: TYK2 Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

TYK2 remains demoted as an active V4 therapeutic target nomination.

Phase 2 verdict: Verdict 3, evidence-driven demotion holds.

## Rationale

The V4 prior-art rule changes the reason for demotion but not the current
status. TYK2 is not P0 target-invalidated, because the local cache does not
show an equivalent progressive-MS or MS biomarker-subgroup TYK2 intervention
that failed clinically with adequate target engagement for target-mechanistic
reasons.

However, V3 evidence did not identify a V4 contribution beyond broad
JAK/IFN/TYK2 pathway inhibition. TYK2 has strong cross-autoimmune genetic and
druggability evidence, but failed the local MS anchor, cell-state/module,
perturbation/model, and same-gene genetics-cell-state overlap gates.
