# Decision 0008: MerTK/TAM Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

`MERTK_TAM` remains demoted as an active therapeutic target nomination. It is
retained as an efferocytosis/resolution comparator and possible biomarker or
future natural-experiment axis.

## V4 Verdict

Verdict 3: evidence-driven demotion holds.

## Rationale

The candidate is not P0 target-invalidated: no locally cached equivalent
correct-direction autoimmune MERTK/TAM agonism failure was found. But V3 failed
to provide the required biology: weak MS single-gene anchor, weak or
contradictory cross-disease recurrence, unresolved MERTK/AXL/GAS6/PROS1
directionality, no convincing perturbation/foundation support, and immature
correct-direction modality.

## Consequence

Do not reopen generic MERTK expression or generic efferocytosis enhancement.
Reopen only with a correct-direction TAM restoration screen showing resolution
or remission biology beyond macrophage abundance in MS plus RA or lupus and at
least one perturbation or natural-experiment dataset.
