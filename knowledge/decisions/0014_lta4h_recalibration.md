# Decision 0014: LTA4H Recalibration

Date: 2026-05-28

## Decision

`LTA4H` remains demoted as a direct therapeutic target. It is retained only as a
lipid-mediator comparator or metabolite/readout axis.

## V4 Verdict

Verdict 3: evidence-driven demotion holds.

## Rationale

The V4 prior-art standard removes "leukotriene biology is crowded" as a
standalone kill. The demotion still holds because V3 found no target-resolved
genetics, no perturbation/foundation support, no FDR-grade replicated MS
response signal, and no single selective intervention node distinct from broad
LTB4/eicosanoid inflammatory biology.

## Consequence

Do not reopen generic LTA4H inhibition or leukotriene blockade. Reopen only with
replicated longitudinal or treatment-response evidence plus target-specific
perturbation or lipidomic flux evidence that is not reducible to generic
myeloid inflammation.
