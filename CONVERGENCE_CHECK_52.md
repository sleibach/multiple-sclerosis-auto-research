# Convergence Check 52: Post-Wave95 Direct-Target Closure

Timestamp: 2026-05-27 20:22 CEST

## Current Track Beliefs

- Accessible/state-marker track: `SEL1L3`, `NRCAM`, `PLEK2`, `C15ORF48`,
  `CD200`, `CHI3L1`, and `ROMO1` are useful for state mapping but not direct
  therapeutic nominations.
- Lipid/efferocytosis route track: `CD300` and `FPR2/ANXA1` remain biologically
  plausible wet-lab kill-test routes, but current MS white-matter support is
  weak or negative.
- Sidecar intervention track: CD58/CD2 and P2RX7 are prior-art blocked; MFGE8
  and FXYD5 require direct wet-lab safety/causality tests; CD82/SEL1L3 are
  markers without actionability.
- Hostile critique track: the main risk is confusing a disease-state marker with
  a transition controller.

## Agreement

All tracks agree that no direct Wave94 or sidecar candidate currently satisfies
the V3 therapeutic-claim bar.

## Disagreement

The only unresolved disagreement is whether `C15ORF48` should be abandoned as a
marker or used as a state anchor to find tractable controllers. The expression
and biology support the latter; the intervention-gate failure rules out direct
targeting.

## Decision

Proceed to Wave96:

- Use `C15ORF48` as a state anchor, not as the intervention target.
- Rank druggable upstream/downstream genes that cohere with the `C15ORF48` state
  across MS and autoimmune tissues.
- Require at least one real perturbation, genetics, targetability, or response
  channel before a controller can be reopened.
