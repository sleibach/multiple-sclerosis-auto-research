# Convergence Check 103 - TNF/IL17-CUX1/NFKBIZ Closure

Timestamp: 2026-05-28 09:14 CEST

## Current Beliefs By Track

Interface perturbation track:
- Human epithelial, keratinocyte, colonic fibroblast, and synovial fibroblast
  systems recurrently induce ELR+ chemokine/interface-entry programs under
  inflammatory stimulation.
- GSE129487 provides a real perturbation signal: CUX1 siRNA suppresses a subset
  of ELR+ chemokines, strongest for CXCL1.

Prior-art and novelty track:
- The CUX1/NFKBIZ/TNF+IL17A stromal synergy mechanism is already directly
  reported by Slowikowski et al. PNAS 2020, DOI 10.1073/pnas.1912702117,
  PubMed 32079724.

MS and cross-disease anchoring track:
- Local V3 artifacts do not provide enough MS anchoring or target-resolved
  genetic support for CUX1/NFKBIZ as a cross-autoimmune therapeutic claim.

Translational track:
- Direct ELR chemokine intervention failed Wave156.
- CUX1/NFKBIZ/STAT3/STAT4 lack a selective reachable modality suitable for a
  V3 translational target claim.

## Agreement

All tracks agree that the inflammatory interface circuit is biologically real.
All tracks also agree that it is not a promotable V3 finding.

## Disagreement

The only remaining ambiguity is whether ELR state could become a predictive
biomarker in a named trial context. No current local dataset supplies that
endpoint, and Epicurus identified this as insufficient for promotion.

## Decision

Close CUX1/NFKBIZ/ELR as target and biomarker routes. Retain the ELR state as a
negative-control/comparator for future interface inflammation analyses. Pivot to
a non-ELR, non-TNF/IL17-synergy intervention candidate.
