## Why This Is Not A Finding

`P2RX7`:

- Broad purine biochemical disturbance exists across multiple diseases.
- Specificity fails against generic inflammatory/APC modules.
- MS, IBD response, RA responder, and target-level gene gates fail.
- Prior RA/Crohn human trials already tested the broad autoimmune hypothesis.

`GPR183`:

- Broad trafficking response modules appear in IBD/RA/Sjogren/T1D contexts.
- Full ligand-production plus direct receptor plus response coherence is absent.
- Ligand-production support is effectively T1D-restricted.
- MS white matter does not support `GPR183`.
- Active GPR183 antagonist clinical/patent prior art covers autoimmune/IBD use.

`EPHX2`:

- Existing raw metabolomics does not contain same-study same-site
  epoxide/diol pairs, so direct sEH activity ratios cannot be computed.
- EPHX2-specific biochemical support is only one supportive disease and one
  normalizing hit.
- Target-level `EPHX2` support is absent across genetics, expression,
  treatment response, and foundation-model screens.
- Broad sEH autoimmune/MS/IBD claims and EAE/IBD model publications block the
  obvious novelty route.

## Decision

Close the current biochemical intervention branch for V3 promotion.

Retained biological memory:

- Cross-autoimmune inflammatory tissue states disturb lipid mediators,
  oxylipins, purines, and trafficking programs.
- These are useful readout and stratification hypotheses, but the available
  data do not nominate a novel, tractable intervention point.

## Next Forcing Question

Move to a different mechanism class. The strongest near-term candidate is not
another lipid mediator target but a genetically anchored inflammatory
macrophage regulatory program that can be tested against local MS/cross-disease
response data and immediately discarded if it collapses into known MEK/NF-kB,
checkpoint, or antigen-presentation prior art.
