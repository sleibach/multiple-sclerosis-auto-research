# V6 Confounder Mining Sidecar

Timestamp: 2026-05-28 20:55 CEST

Scope: identify V3-V5 cases where an apparent signal collapsed, weakened, or
changed interpretation under covariate adjustment or component decomposition,
then convert the biologically plausible absorbed variable into Tier -1
hypotheses/refinements. This report makes no therapeutic claims.

## Operating Rule Used

`meta/TIER_MINUS_1_RULEBOOK.md` explicitly treats confounders as productive:
if a signal disappears after adjustment, the adjusted-out variable becomes a
hypothesis if biologically plausible. I applied that rule below.

## Cross-Case Synthesis

The same failure pattern recurs across V3-V5:

1. Single-gene targets often collapsed into broader biological states
   (`ACSL1` into lipid-lysosomal myeloid state; `CD74` into APC/HLA-II/size;
   `NAMPT` into HIF/NAD stress metabolism).
2. Bulk blood signals often collapsed into composition or source-mix variables
   (`GSE17410` IFN/APC into hematologic composition; pDC markers diverging from
   ISG).
3. Treatment-response signals often collapsed into state remodeling rather than
   target-specific behavior (`GSE282122` HLA-II/CD74 into IFN/APC).
4. These collapses are not dead ends at Tier -1. They define better objects:
   state controllers, source switches, trafficking readiness, repair-versus-
   activation splits, and residual outlier compartments.

Priority Tier -1 refinements for immediate follow-up:

1. MS late-pregnancy hematologic/source-switch axis.
2. APC-state-controller axis replacing direct MIF/CD74.
3. OPC/astrocyte lesion-stress residual CD74/HLA-II-like states.
4. Anti-TNF IFN/APC remodeling axis.
5. Lipid-lysosomal controller axis replacing ACSL1/LTA4H single-gene framing.
