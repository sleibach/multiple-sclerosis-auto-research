# Convergence Check 83 - Response Biomarker Salvage

Timestamp: 2026-05-28 10:24 CEST

## Question

Can treatment-response data rescue a translational route after target nomination
branches fail?

## Evidence

- Wave129 identified two biomarker-only anti-TNF nonresponse signals:
  `IL1B` and `LAMP3`.
- Both replicate directionally across IBD and RA anti-TNF contexts under the
  local gates.
- Neither is eligible as a target nomination:
  - `IL1B` is prior-art crowded and closed as direct intervention.
  - `LAMP3` is a marker-state node, not a druggable controller.
- Neither carries the MS response context needed for an MS-centered V3 claim.

## Decision

Keep IL1B/LAMP3 as response-state biomarkers only.

## Next Forcing Question

Determine whether an MS treatment-response dataset exists locally or can be
accessed cheaply. Without MS response evidence, this cannot become a V3
stratification finding.
