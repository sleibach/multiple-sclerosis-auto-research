No cross-autoimmune genetically anchored and druggable alternate axis is promoted.

The best genetics-first alternatives outside the exhausted lipid-lysosomal/APC
module are real biology, but every one fails at least one hard gate:

1. no current modality in the autoimmune-correct direction;

## Candidate Notes

### `PTPN2`

This is the strongest non-excluded genetics-first candidate by local score:
8 local OpenTargets diseases at score >=0.5, broad h5ad positives in Crohn,
psoriasis, T1D, and UC, and no broad h5ad negative disease. Public literature
supports PTPN2/TCPTP as a negative regulator of inflammatory signaling and
barrier integrity in IBD and autoimmunity.

Failure: the therapeutic direction is restoration of TCPTP function. ChEMBL and
oncology drug discovery show inhibitor/degrader feasibility, not autoimmune-safe
activation. A PTPN2 inhibitor would be directionally unsafe for the genetic
story.

Key public sources:

- PTPN2 and IBD review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4716018/
- PTPN2 loss-of-function and inflammation/immunity review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9456094/
- PTPN2/PTPN1 inhibitor oncology paper: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06575-7

Data needed to reopen: multi-disease disease-GWAS/cis-eQTL coloc showing that
lower PTPN2 function mediates risk in the relevant cells, plus a genuine TCPTP
restorer/activator or allele-correcting modality with disease-cell perturbation
showing reduced inflammatory injury without impairing epithelial or Treg
homeostasis.

### `GPR65`

This was the most tempting alternate axis because it is a membrane GPCR and the
local OT file gives 5 diseases at score >=0.5 (`AS`, Crohn, MS, psoriasis, UC).
The ChEMBL API returned a human GPR65 target and 99 activity records. The
mechanistic model is also outside the exhausted APC module: acidic tissue
microenvironment sensing through GPR65/TDAG8 and cAMP.

Failure: local disease-cell support is weak and contradictory in
`broad_h5ad_gene_rank.tsv` (1 positive disease, 2 negative diseases), and the
specific IBD/GPR65 therapeutic idea is already in the literature and patents.

Key public sources:

- GPR65 experimental colitis target paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8629932/
- GPR65 IBD variant functional paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6323805/
- GPR65 modulator patent WO2023067322A1: https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2023067322A1/en

Data needed to reopen: fine-mapped multi-disease colocalization in monocytes or
relevant epithelial compartments, a selective agonist/PAM with in vivo exposure,
and a non-IBD autoimmune perturbation package showing a new biomarker-defined
population not covered by existing GPR65 modulator claims.

### `CLEC16A` / `ATG16L1`

These are the credible autophagy/mitophagy alternatives. The local genetics are
broad (`CLEC16A`: 7 diseases; `ATG16L1`: 8 diseases at OT score >=0.5), and
CLEC16A public biology links mitophagy defects to autoimmune and neurologic
phenotypes.

Failure: neither is directly druggable now. `CLEC16A` also sits in a complicated
16p13 region with `CIITA`, `DEXI`, and `SOCS1`, so locus breadth is not
target-level causality.

Key public sources:

- CLEC16A autoimmunity/mitophagy review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10179542/
- CLEC16A astrocyte/MS model paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12039076/
- CLEC16A/DEXI/CIITA/SOCS1 locus ambiguity: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3634488/

Data needed to reopen: causal fine mapping separating `CLEC16A` from neighboring
genes, direct CLEC16A target engagement or a precisely justified downstream
mitophagy modality, and disease-cell rescue experiments in at least two
non-IBD autoimmune contexts.

### `SH2B3`

`SH2B3` has the broadest local genetic breadth (10 OT diseases at score >=0.5)
and clear negative-regulatory cytokine biology.

Failure: the 12q24 locus is strongly pleiotropic, local expression support is
weak, and LNK restoration is not directly druggable. It also overlaps generic
cytokine/JAK logic without a clean modality.

Key public sources:

- 12q24/SH2B3 pleiotropy review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4058736/
- LNK/JAK-STAT hematopoiesis review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8781068/
- Rare SH2B3 lupus variant functional paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10901239/

Data needed to reopen: target-resolved coloc/MR independent of neighboring
12q24 effects, a direct restoration modality, and disease-cell perturbation
showing cytokine dampening without hematopoietic/platelet liability.

### `IRF5`

`IRF5` is genetically broad and biologically strong, with 9 local OT diseases at
score >=0.5. It is no longer undruggable in principle: public 2026 materials
describe oral allosteric inhibitor programs, and IRF5 degrader programs are
also in motion.

Failure: this is already direct lupus/autoimmune therapeutic prior art and is
close to the generic TLR/type-I-IFN axis excluded by the prompt. No materially
new compartment or biomarker population delta emerged from the local tables.

Key public sources:

- IRF5 inhibitor lupus-model paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7685739/
- Genetic/chemical IRF5 inhibition in mouse lupus: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8290003/
- HotSpot 2026 IRF5 inhibitor program: https://www.hotspotthera.com/press_release/hotspot-therapeutics-presents-preclinical-data-from-small-molecule-irf5-inhibitor-program-at-15th-european-lupus-meeting/
- Kymera IRF5 degrader public materials: https://investors.kymeratx.com/node/11946/pdf

Data needed to reopen: a non-lupus, non-generic-IFN biomarker-defined autoimmune
population with direct human perturbation evidence and freedom-to-operate beyond
existing IRF5 inhibitor/degrader programs.

## Final Call

Wave20 does not identify a promotable alternate axis. The negative result is not
because there is no cross-autoimmune genetics. There is plenty of it. The
blocker is the intersection of target-level specificity, correct-direction
druggability, perturbation support, and novelty.

Most actionable next data:

1. Full summary-stat multi-signal coloc for `PTPN2`, `GPR65`, `CLEC16A`,
   `SH2B3`, and `IRF5` across MS, IBD, psoriasis, RA/SLE, T1D, and celiac/PBC
   where available.
2. Cell-type eQTL or pQTL instruments from immune, epithelial, thyroid, CNS,
   and gut tissues, not just GTEx presence/absence.
3. Correct-direction perturbation assays: PTPN2/A20/LNK/CLEC16A restoration,
   GPR65 agonism/PAM, and non-lupus IRF5 inhibition in human disease-relevant
   cells.
4. Freedom-to-operate and novelty review before any reopened candidate is
   promoted.
