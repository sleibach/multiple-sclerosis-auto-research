## Decision

No candidate passes the intervention-grade bar after independent prior-closure and druggability audit.

Minimum bar used here:

- target-resolved causal direction, not only locus association or L2G/QTL priority;
- cross-disease autoimmune relevance plus a strict MS/local or module-state anchor;
- disease-relevant direct perturbation or model evidence in the correct direction;
- credible modality with safety and delivery logic;
- no blocking prior-art saturation for the same intervention axis.

Every ranked survivor fails at least one hard blocker, and the superficially druggable rows fail because their druggability comes from already-crowded or directionally unsafe immune axes. The residual unblocked category is therefore empty for Wave83A.
