# Ankylosing Spondylitis / SpA Report: Planck

Returned: 2026-05-26 19:11 UTC

## Verdict

A lysosomal antigen-processing inflammatory myeloid state is most convincing in
joint/synovial-fluid SpA compartments, especially AS SFMC and PsA synovial
fluid. It is not yet cleanly proven in AS gut or enthesis tissue, and blood
evidence is mixed.

## Evidence Summary

- AS synovial fluid `GSE216883/GSE216885`: estimated AS SFMC myeloid cells show
  high detection for `IFI30`, `CTSB`, `CD74`, `HLA-DRA`, `STAT1`, `IRF1`, and
  `NAMPT`. An inflammatory antigen-core was reported at about 84% of estimated
  SFMC myeloid cells versus about 57% in PBMC.
- PsA/SpA synovial fluid `GSE161500`: estimated myeloid cells were about 46-67%
  of cells; antigen/inflammatory core was about 46-65% of estimated myeloid
  cells. HLA-DRA/CD74/CTSB were near-universal; `IFI30`, `STAT1`, and `NAMPT`
  were frequent.
- Bulk synovial biopsy `GSE41038` is useful but tiny and weak.
- Blood evidence is mixed: `GSE194315` supports monocyte HLA signals, while
  `GSE11886` reports a reverse IFN signature in AS-derived macrophages.
- Drug-response evidence is unresolved: `GSE141646` paired PBMC RNA-seq had
  mixed CTSD/CTSB down and HLA-II up after TNFi.
- Enthesis and gut evidence is not yet a direct proof of this state.

## Integration Decision

Count SpA/PsA as strong support for the inflammatory APC/myeloid state in
synovial fluid, not as pan-tissue proof across all AS disease compartments.
