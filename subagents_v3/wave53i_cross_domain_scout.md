# Wave53-I Cross-Domain Intervention Scout

Status: completed and closed.

## Verdict

No therapeutic shortlist.

One low-confidence wet-lab reopener survived as testable but not promotable:
`MFGE8` augmentation. The other adjacent-domain mechanisms are no-go
comparators.

## 1. MFGE8 / Integrin-Mediated Debris-Opsonin Augmentation

Why not already closed:

- Not `MERTK`, `TREM2`, `CD300`, or `FPR2`.
- Soluble phosphatidylserine/myelin-debris bridge.

Anchors flagged by the subagent:

- `MFG-E8` promotes microglial myelin-debris uptake and remyelination in a
  non-MS model.
- `MFG-E8` deficiency links defective apoptotic-cell handling to autoimmunity.
- `MFG-E8` can also promote inflammatory phagocytosis of viable neurons.

Local check:

- `MFGE8` has one local disease positive and a nominal MS trend, not FDR
  support:
  `results_v3/broad_h5ad_gene_discovery/broad_h5ad_gene_rank.tsv`.
- CRISPR efferocytosis screen was unresolved:
  `results_v3/wave37_gse212008_crispr_efferocytosis_screen/gene_level_screen_scores.tsv`.

Impression: `PARK_EX_VIVO_ONLY`.

## 3. LAIR1 Agonism / C1q-Collagen Inhibitory Receptor

## 4. HLA-G to LILRB2/ILT4 Tolerogenic APC Agonism

## 5. VSIG4/CRIg Macrophage Inhibitory/Complement Checkpoint

Reason: local V3 data are actively unfavorable, including negative MS
white-matter signal and Crohn/UC negative direction.
