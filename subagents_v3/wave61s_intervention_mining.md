# Wave61-S Intervention Mining Audit

Working rule: promote only perturbation-first evidence. Expression overlap, disease-cell correlation, and L1000 reversal can support context, but they do not nominate a therapeutic route unless a real intervention changes the relevant readout with selectivity and guardrails.

## Verdict

No candidate in the inspected V3 perturbation artifacts clears promotion. The best real perturbation-first biology is `MED16/Mediator` and `GSK3B` knockout in primary mouse macrophages, but both remain non-promotable: `MED16` is a wet-lab comparator with broad-transcription risk, and `GSK3B` is pleiotropic/prior-art crowded. The next real perturbation signals (`TNFRSF1A`, `RFX5`, `CHUK`) come from Mixscale CRISPRi in stimulated cancer cell lines and are blocked by MS safety, host-defense, or broad NF-kB/MHC-II liabilities. L1000-only hits, generic JAK/IFN collapse, steroids, cytotoxic/oncology mechanisms, unresolved BRD compounds, and direct lysosomal/sphingolipid enzyme hits should stay demoted.

## Bottom Line

The perturbation-first audit supports one mechanistic statement: the antigen-presentation/lipid-lysosomal myeloid readout can be moved by real perturbation, most convincingly by `Med16_KO` and secondarily by `Gsk3b_KO`. It does not yet support a therapeutic intervention. Promotion requires a new experiment, not another expression rank: a tractable perturbation in primary human myeloid cells or MS lesion slice/organoid context must selectively reduce the lipid-lysosomal/APC module, avoid generic IFN/NF-kB/JAK collapse, preserve viability/stress guardrails, and either improve or not impair efferocytosis/repair.
