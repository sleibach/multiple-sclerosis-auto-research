## Bottom Line

Broad IFI30/GILT inhibition, activation, or "modulation for autoimmunity/MS" is
not cleanly novel. MS/EAE and RA have the closest blocking art. For IBD,
psoriasis, Sjogren, PBC, celiac, and SLE, no direct IFI30-targeted therapeutic
trial or clean IFI30 inhibitor/activator patent was found in the requested
searches, but IFI30/GILT is already prior-arted as part of IFN/MHC-II/APC
disease biology or biomarker panels.

A narrow biomarker claim can still survive, but only if framed as a cell-state
or tissue-state companion/enrichment biomarker using combined
`IFN-gamma/HLA-II/CD74/IFI30-GILT` to predict response to JAK/IFN/
antigen-processing interventions. It should not be framed as IFI30 alone,
generic IFN signature, MS activity diagnosis, or CD74/MIF modulation.

## Intervention Direction

IFI30/GILT inhibition is risky and partly blocked: EAE GILT loss changes peptide
repertoire and can switch pathology rather than simply suppress disease. Do not
claim "GILT inhibition treats MS" broadly.

IFI30/GILT activation is also not clean: GILT is required for an antigen-specific
tolerogenic MOG construct, and a 2025 autoimmune optic neuritis paper reports
STAT1 inhibition acting through IFI30 upregulation:
[PMID 40152123](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40152123/). That creates
activation/upregulation prior art in CNS autoimmunity.

Generic "modulation" is blocked by reviews describing GILT as an
IFN-gamma-induced lysosomal reductase shaping MHC-II peptidomes and autoimmunity:
[PMC3885806](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3885806/),
[PMC4287230](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4287230/).

## Biomarker Novelty

Prior art is heavy for IFN signatures, JAK-response biology, HLA-II antigen
presentation, CD74, and IFI30 separately. Examples: IFN gene signature as an
autoimmune rheumatic biomarker, RA JAK inhibitors suppress IFN-regulated genes
and IFN signature proposed for JAK-inhibitor personalization, and SLE
biomarker-stratified biologics meta-analysis including IGS/JAK/anti-IFN classes.

What survives: the exact combined `IFN-gamma + HLA-II + IFI30/GILT + CD74`
tissue/cell-state biomarker used prospectively to stratify JAK/IFN/
antigen-processing interventions. The strongest claim is treatment-by-biomarker
interaction, not diagnostic enrichment.
