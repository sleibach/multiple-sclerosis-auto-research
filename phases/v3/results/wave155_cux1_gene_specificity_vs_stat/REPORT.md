# Wave155 CUX1 Gene Specificity Versus STAT

Branch call: `CUX1_HAS_NOMINAL_NONSTAT_INTERFACE_GENE_SUBSET`.

This wave tests individual genes in the recurrent interface modules. A CUX1-selective nominal gene-context is induced under control siRNA, suppressed by CUX1 siRNA at p<0.05, and not suppressed by STAT3 or STAT4 siRNA at p<0.05 in the same context.
