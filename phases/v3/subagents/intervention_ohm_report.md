# Intervention Scout Report: Ohm

Returned: 2026-05-26 19:17 UTC

## Ranked Shortlist

### 1. CD74/MIF Axis: Best Keep

Druggability is credible through MIF small molecules and anti-CD74 biologics,
but CD74 depletion should not be the lead concept. Ibudilast is practical
starting matter because of CNS exposure and progressive MS human data, though it
is not a clean MIF-only probe.

Pros:

- CNS/MS relevance is strongest among candidates; SPRINT-MS reported reduced
  brain atrophy with follow-up imaging support, though not a clean disability
  win.
- CD74 recurs across Sjogren, AITD, T1D, MG, SpA, celiac, PBC and HLA-II/APC
  states.
- Lead indication could be progressive MS with CD74/MIF-high myeloid lesions.

Failure modes:

- Ibudilast is polypharmacology.
- MIF may have compensatory or repair roles.
- CD74 blockade risks broad APC/B-cell effects.
- MS ibudilast patents/trials and anti-CD74 autoimmune patents/literature are
  substantial.

### 2. CTSS-Selective Lysosomal APC Inhibition: Keep, Heavily Haircutted

CTSS is the most druggable lysosomal APC enzyme. It is assayable and more
selective than `CTSB/CTSD`, but direct clinical baggage is substantial.

Pros:

- Enzymatic and druggable.
- Mechanistically tied to MHC-II peptide loading.
- Fits PBC/SpA/celiac/MG and broader lysosomal APC arm.

Failure modes:

- RO5459072/petesicatib missed the primary Sjogren endpoint.
- Celiac gluten-challenge study found no clear treatment effect.
- Reports also cite psoriasis and RA misses.
- Survives only as biomarker-defined/delivery-optimized strategy, not as generic
  "CTSS inhibits MHC-II" novelty.

### 3. TREM1: Conditional Peripheral Keep

Good biologic tractability and peripheral delivery, but weak MS relevance and
thin V3 local support. Best lead would be TREM1-high IBD incomplete responders.
AbbVie/Celsius likely blocks the clean antibody path in IBD.

### 4. IFN/JAK/STAT1/IRF1 Tuning: Benchmark, Not New Lead

Excellent druggability but poor selectivity and saturated prior art. Use as ex
vivo perturbation control, not as proprietary V3 therapeutic scout.

## Reject/Demote

- `CXCL10/CXCR3`: strong signal but bad target; redundancy, prior clinical
  baggage, weak CNS delivery.
- `IFI30/GILT`: excellent biology/biomarker, poor intervention because it is an
  intracellular lysosomal reductase with no mature drug matter.
- `NAMPT/HIF`: reject systemic intracellular NAMPT/HIF inhibition; toxicity and
  broad prior art.
- `TGM2/LRP1/HLA-DQ`: excellent celiac-specific circuit, not pan-autoimmune.
- `OSM/OSMR`: deprioritize; local V3 support too narrow and UC trial issues.
- `C1Q/C1QBP/SARM1`: not cross-autoimmune; lupus biology contradicts naive C1q
  suppression.

## Integration Decision

Best therapeutic path is a stratified CD74/MIF-high progressive MS program with
cross-autoimmune APC-state support, using CTSS as a mechanistic comparator and
biomarker arm rather than the primary novelty claim.
