## Scope

Audited Wave86 external anti-TNF nonresponse genes/circuit:
`IL1B`, `CXCL8`, `TREM1`, `CCL3/CCL4/CCL2`, `OSM`, `SPP1`,
`LAMP3`, `IFI30`, `ACSL1`.

Wave86 signal being audited: baseline mucosal nonresponders to anti-TNF
in `GSE12251`, `GSE14580`, and `GSE16879` had higher inflammatory /
myeloid genes, with strongest gene-level anchors including `IL1B`,
`CXCL8`, `TREM1`, `CCL3/4/2`, `OSM`, `SPP1`, `LAMP3`, `IFI30`, and
`ACSL1`. This audit asks whether a specific intervention point in that
gene set is still open after literature, trial, and patent checks.

## Ranking By Practical Translational Openness

1. `ACSL1`: least prior-art-blocked among audited genes, with actual
   selective chemical matter, but still not repurposing-ready and not
   proven causal in anti-TNF resistance.
2. `IFI30`: less patent-blocked, but direct inhibition is mechanistically
   risky because it perturbs antigen processing and peptide repertoire.
3. `LAMP3`: potentially useful as a tissue-local DC-state biomarker, not
   a tractable target.
4. `CXCL8/CXCR2`: druggable, but crowded and likely nonspecific
   neutrophil-inflammation biology.
5. `IL1B`, `CCL/CCR`, `SPP1`, `TREM1`, `OSM`: blocked by existing
   trials, patents, failed adjacent programs, or direct anti-TNF
   nonresponse prior art.

## Final Call

`NO_UNBLOCKED_INTERVENTION_POINT_FROM_WAVE86_LIST`.

The Wave86 genes are credible markers of a tissue inflammatory myeloid /
neutrophil-chemoattractant anti-TNF nonresponse state, especially in IBD
mucosa. They do not currently yield a clean new intervention point. The
two most mechanistically attractive drug targets, `OSM/OSMR` and
`TREM1`, are directly blocked by prior anti-TNF-response literature,
active or recent clinical programs, and patents. The least blocked
candidate, `ACSL1`, is not blocked mainly because it is not yet
translationally mature; it should be treated as a parked macrophage
metabolic-state hypothesis, not as a Wave87 therapeutic nomination.

Recommended orchestrator use: keep Wave86 as a stratification and
mechanism-mapping result; do not promote any listed gene as the V3
intervention point without a separate causal perturbation and selectivity
package.
