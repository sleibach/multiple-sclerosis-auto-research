# CD74/MIF Novelty And Translation Audit: Galileo

Returned: 2026-05-26 19:43 UTC

## Audit Result

The broad concept is blocked: MIF/CD74-axis modulation in progressive MS,
including ibudilast, is already published and patented.

The narrower concept that survives is a predictive/enrichment biomarker
program: selecting progressive MS patients with a
`CD74/CD44/CXCR4/HLA-II`-high myeloid receptor state for MIF/CD74-axis
modulation. This is incremental stratification, not a new target or new
ibudilast use.

## Direct Prior Art

- Fox et al., NEJM 2018 / SPRINT-MS / `NCT01982942`: ibudilast in PPMS/SPMS,
  255 randomized, 96 weeks, slower brain atrophy; ibudilast crosses the BBB and
  is described as inhibiting MIF, PDEs, and TLR4.
- Ladakis et al., Mult Scler 2024: baseline serum MIF predicted faster brain
  atrophy in PPMS but not SPMS; ibudilast did not reduce serum or CSF MIF.
- Nakamura et al., Mult Scler 2024: post hoc SPRINT-MS analysis; ibudilast
  reduced slowly enlarging lesion volume by 23%.
- Benedek et al., PNAS 2017: MIF, D-DT, and CD74 linked to MS
  severity/progression, especially male progressive disease; suggests targeting
  MIF/CD74 in selected MS patients.
- Journal of Neuroimmunology 2018: MIF/DDT receptors `CD74`, `CD44`, `CXCR2`,
  `CXCR4` implicated in EAE/MS; ISO-1 improved EAE.
- 2026 cancer/neuroinflammation prior art: MIF-induced `CD74+`
  microglia/macrophages, coexpression of `CXCR4/CD44`, noncanonical CD74
  myeloid program across CNS disorders including MS, and ibudilast as a
  brain-penetrant MIF-CD74 inhibitor.

## Novelty That May Survive

Potentially novel but narrow:

Use a combined `CD74/CD44/CXCR4/HLA-II`-high myeloid receptor state as a
predictive enrichment biomarker to identify progressive MS patients most likely
to respond to MIF/CD74-axis modulation.

What appears not directly published:

- treatment-by-biomarker interaction analysis for this receptor state in
  SPRINT-MS or another progressive MS cohort;
- SEL/PRL/atrophy response prediction from a lesion/CSF receptor-state assay;
- CSF/lesion receptor-state companion diagnostic distinct from serum MIF alone.

## Blocking Patents And Trials

- `US11083713B2`: ibudilast in progressive MS, including PPMS/SPMS.
- `WO2007142924A1`: ibudilast for inhibiting MIF activity.
- `CA3174413A1` / `WO2021207054` family: reducing plasma MIF with ibudilast.
- `US10525101B2`: MIF-binding polypeptides and autoimmune disorders including
  MS.
- `US9643922B2`: MIF modulator concepts including CD44/CD74 interaction.
- `NCT01982942`: ibudilast progressive MS, completed.
- Anti-CD74 precedent outside MS: milatuzumab in SLE, `NCT01845740`.

## Translational Implication

Ibudilast is feasible pharmacologically but not mechanistically selective. A
future program should either:

- pursue retrospective SPRINT-MS treatment-by-biomarker interaction using
  `CD74/CD44/CXCR4/HLA-II` myeloid state, serum/CSF MIF/DDT/sCD74, and
  SEL/atrophy outcomes; or
- avoid ibudilast as the centerpiece and develop/select a cleaner CNS-penetrant
  MIF/CD74 functional inhibitor with target-engagement biomarkers.
