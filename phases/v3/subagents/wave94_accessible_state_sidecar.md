## Executive Call

No non-lipid accessible or state-transition candidate is target-ready.

The least bad reopenable candidates are `CD58/CD2`, `FXYD5`, and `MFGE8`, but each is only assay- or comparator-grade. `CD58/CD2` has the strongest local evidence but is blocked by prior art and direction. `FXYD5` is the cleanest underexplored accessible molecule, but only a non-depleting, barrier-preserving perturbation can reopen it. `MFGE8` has the cleanest mechanistic assay concept, but the local autoimmune/MS evidence is weak. `P2RX7`, `CD82`, `CD200`, `P4HB`, and `SEL1L3` do not justify promotion from current local evidence.

## Explicit Non-Reopen Notes

- `SPNS1`: biologically interesting lysosomal transport/egress comparator, but Wave39 has no MS anchor (`ms_wm_delta_log2=-0.104499`, p `0.483457`), no exact ChEMBL activity, and Wave83 marks `SPNS1_TARGETABILITY` as `NO_GO_INTERVENTION_CLASS_META_RANK` with reachable modality `0`, MS anchor `0`, genetic/target resolution `0`, response support `0`.
- `C15ORF48`: strong expression/state signal but no clear accessible drug modality in the requested artifacts; Wave71 meta-score `-2.2` and no target-resolved coloc/MR. This is a state marker/mechanism clue, not an intervention point from current local files.
- `NRCAM`: accessible and parked in Wave94, but neural adhesion/safety and weak genetics/modality make it lower priority than `SEL1L3` for this sidecar.
- Direct lipid-controller routes from Waves91/92 remain closed. Wave91 module-wide lipid-lysosomal intervention rank reports no reopened module-wide lipid intervention; Wave92 controller-route rank makes `CD300_RECEPTOR_SPECIFIC_TUNING` the best wet-lab-only lipid/efferocytosis comparator, not a non-lipid accessible target claim.

## Bottom Line

Promote nothing from this sidecar.

Actionable next tests, in order:

1. Use `CD58/CD2` only as a benchmark/stratification comparator because biology is strong but prior art and direction block target novelty.
2. Run one bounded `FXYD5` kill test only if a non-depleting, barrier-preserving perturbation assay is available.
3. Run `MFGE8` only as an ex vivo repair/efferocytosis safety assay, not as a cross-autoimmune target claim.
4. Keep `P2RX7`, `CD82`, `CD200`, `P4HB`, and `SEL1L3` parked unless new target-level perturbation or genetics arrives.
