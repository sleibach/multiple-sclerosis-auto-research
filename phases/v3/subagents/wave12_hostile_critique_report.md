## Hard Veto

The broad residual-gate pivot is not ready for promotion. It looks like a
screening artifact generated after multiple previous hypotheses failed, not a
coherent target-discovery result.

The named top candidates are mostly not in the lipid-lysosomal myeloid
neighborhood. They are dominated by stromal, epithelial, barrier, matrix,
complement, hypoxia, and generic injury biology. Most "strict" residual-gate
survivors are still only strict within IBD stromal contexts. That is not
cross-autoimmune causal support.

Do not write a `FINDING_V3` around `ATOX1`, `SQLE`, `TPM4`, `LDLRAD3`,
`C1QTNF1`, `HIF1A`, `CBX3`, `CFB`, or `TIMP1`. At most, this pivot can be
described as a negative control showing that broad residualization still
returns generic diseased-tissue programs.

## Central Failure

The pivot has changed the biological object again. Earlier waves demoted the
lipid-loader myeloid module, canonical IFN/APC, `LIPA`, OSMR/complement,
`LTA4H`/`CHI3L1`, `APOC1`, `SNX10`, and `C15ORF48`. The new broad residual
gate now elevates copper handling, cholesterol synthesis, tropomyosin,
LDL-receptor-like annotation, complement, hypoxia, chromatin, and fibrosis
genes. That is not a refined mechanism. It is hypothesis drift.

The strongest rows are not MS-centered. For the requested candidates, only
`CBX3` has a nominal MS white-matter flag, and even that is a modest expression
signal, not a causal perturbation result. `ATOX1`, `SQLE`, `TPM4`, `LDLRAD3`,
`C1QTNF1`, `HIF1A`, `CFB`, and `TIMP1` have no nominal MS positive flag in the
broad residual table. A V3 autoimmune target cannot be presented as relevant to
the original MS/autonomous-autoimmune goal if the MS anchor is absent or
nominal only.

## Candidate-Level Attacks

### `ATOX1`

`ATOX1` survives mainly in IBD stromal rows and psoriasis skin stromal. It is
not in the lipid-lysosomal myeloid neighborhood and has no nominal MS support
in the table.

False-positive mechanisms:

- Copper/oxidative-stress handling can mark inflamed, stressed, or remodeling
  stromal tissue without being disease causal.
- Stromal positivity can reflect fibroblast activation, vascular remodeling,
  or wound repair rather than immune-state control.
- Residual association with `ifn_apc`, `hla_ii_apc`, and complement modules may
  mean it tracks inflamed tissue neighborhoods, not an independent mechanism.

Promotion would require independent stromal-cell replication outside IBD,
single-cell spatial localization to pathogenic stromal niches, perturbation
showing disease-module normalization, and safety counterscreens for copper
homeostasis and oxidative stress.

### `SQLE`

`SQLE` is a cholesterol-biosynthesis enzyme, but the table does not place it in
the local lipid-lysosomal myeloid neighborhood. Its retained support is again
IBD stromal plus psoriasis stromal, with no MS flag.

False-positive mechanisms:

- Cholesterol synthesis can rise with cell growth, epithelial/stromal repair,
  hypoxia, and inflammatory remodeling.
- Statin-pathway or sterol-pathway plausibility is not target novelty.
- A stromal `SQLE` signal is not evidence for macrophage lipid-lysosomal
  disease control.

Promotion would require showing that `SQLE` perturbation in the relevant
primary autoimmune cell state changes pathogenic inflammatory output at doses
that do not simply impair proliferation, viability, or membrane biogenesis.

### `TPM4`

`TPM4` has many raw positive rows, but that breadth is a liability. It appears
in IBD epithelial, myeloid, and stromal compartments plus psoriasis stromal.
That is compatible with cytoskeletal remodeling across injured tissues, not a
specific autoimmune target.

False-positive mechanisms:

- Actin/tropomyosin programs rise during epithelial restitution, stromal
  contraction, migration, and tissue repair.
- Cross-compartment positivity can reflect shared damage mechanics rather than
  shared immune causality.
- It has no nominal MS support and is outside the lipid-lysosomal myeloid
  neighborhood.

Promotion would require perturbation separating pathogenic remodeling from
normal repair and motility. Without that, `TPM4` is a repair-state marker.

### `LDLRAD3`

`LDLRAD3` is superficially attractive because of LDL-receptor-like naming, but
the local result does not support a lipid-lysosomal myeloid interpretation. It
is mainly IBD stromal/myeloid plus psoriasis APC/stromal, with strict
survivors only in IBD stromal.

False-positive mechanisms:

- Receptor-like annotation can create lipid-story confirmation bias.
- Stromal and APC associations may reflect tissue injury and inflammatory
  cell-neighborhood composition.
- The large UC myeloid raw effect is not enough if strict covariate survival
  is stromal/IBD-bound.

Promotion would require direct functional evidence of ligand, pathway, and
direction in disease-relevant primary cells, plus independent non-IBD
replication.

### `C1QTNF1`

`C1QTNF1` has only two raw positive analyses, both IBD stromal, despite the
table assigning broad discovery reasons. It has no non-IBD retained positive
analysis and no MS flag.

False-positive mechanisms:

- Adipokine/complement-like gene labels invite complement-story overreach.
- Stromal IBD signal can reflect fibrosis, mesenchymal activation, or
  inflamed-mucosa architecture.
- Low analysis count makes the row fragile to dataset and annotation choices.

Promotion would require replication in at least two non-IBD diseases in the
same cell class and a directional perturbation that does not merely change
fibroblast activation or extracellular-matrix remodeling.

### `HIF1A`

`HIF1A` is a generic stress regulator, not a clean target. The table shows
IBD stromal, epithelial, and myeloid associations, with strict survivors in IBD
stromal only. There is no MS flag and no neighborhood membership.

False-positive mechanisms:

- Hypoxia is a near-universal feature of inflamed, damaged, and metabolically
  stressed tissue.
- `HIF1A` covariance with lysosomal/APC/complement modules can be a severity
  readout, not an upstream actionable node.
- Direct `HIF1A` modulation risks broad effects on barrier repair, metabolism,
  angiogenesis, macrophage function, and tissue adaptation.

Promotion would require causality in primary disease tissue, a narrow
intervention modality, and proof that changing `HIF1A` improves pathogenic
immune programs without blocking adaptive hypoxia responses.

### `CBX3`

`CBX3` is the best-looking row only because it has a nominal MS positive flag
and several non-IBD retained positives. That does not make it a target.
Chromatin factors are almost maximally vulnerable to generic state,
proliferation, stress, and cell-composition confounding.

False-positive mechanisms:

- Chromatin-associated expression can track activation, replication,
  differentiation, or cell-cycle-adjacent state shifts.
- The strict core covariate survivor is only UC stromal.
- The MS white-matter signal is nominal expression, not disease-module
  reversal or genetic causality.

Promotion would require allele/genetic support, disease-cell-specific
perturbation, chromatin target engagement, and severe safety counterscreens.
Without a selective modality and directionality, `CBX3` should be treated as a
state marker.

### `CFB`

`CFB` is plausible complement biology but not novel enough or specific enough.
The broad table shows many raw positives across T1D, psoriasis, and IBD, but
only one strict core covariate survivor: Crohn stromal. No MS flag.

False-positive mechanisms:

- Complement genes rise downstream of tissue injury, epithelial stress,
  macrophage infiltration, and local inflammation.
- `CFB` in epithelial/stromal compartments can mark local complement
  activation rather than a causal targetable driver.
- Complement intervention is heavily prior-arted and indication-specific;
  residual expression alone does not identify a new autoimmune target.

Promotion would require disease-specific complement activation evidence,
genetic/colocalization support, protein-level target engagement, and clear
separation from host-defense and tissue-clearance functions.

### `TIMP1`

`TIMP1` is one of the clearest false-positive traps. It is a matrix-remodeling,
fibrosis, wound-repair, and inflammatory-damage gene. It is high in UC
stromal/myeloid/epithelial rows plus psoriasis keratinocyte and T1D pancreatic
stromal/acinar contexts, but strict survival is only UC stromal.

False-positive mechanisms:

- It can be a scar, wound, fibrosis, and tissue-destruction readout.
- Cross-compartment signal is exactly what matrix-repair markers do in
  inflamed tissue.
- Modulating `TIMP1` risks worsening matrix turnover, fibrosis, epithelial
  repair, and tissue integrity.

Promotion would require proving that `TIMP1` is pathogenic rather than a repair
or containment response, with longitudinal tissue and perturbation evidence.

## Broad False-Positive Mechanisms

- **IBD stromal dominance:** many strict survivors are `ibd_crohn_stromal` or
  `ibd_uc_stromal`; this can reflect inflamed mucosal remodeling rather than
  cross-autoimmune biology.
- **Residualization leakage:** residualizing against selected modules does not
  remove all severity, sampling, hypoxia, matrix, epithelial injury, and
  composition effects.
- **Compartment mixing:** epithelial, stromal, APC, myeloid, keratinocyte,
  acinar, stellate, and microglial readouts are being treated as interchangeable
  evidence.
- **Repair-state capture:** `TPM4`, `TIMP1`, `CFB`, `ATOX1`, `SQLE`, and
  `C1QTNF1` can all plausibly rise during tissue repair, remodeling, or stress.
- **Annotation-driven storytelling:** LDL-like, complement-like, cholesterol,
  hypoxia, and chromatin labels provide easy narratives after selection.
- **Weak MS anchoring:** the new pivot mostly lacks MS support, and a nominal
  MS expression flag is not a therapeutic anchor.
- **Selection after failure:** after many demotions, broadening the gate
  increases the chance of discovering a statistical survivor rather than a
  biologically central target.
- **No directionality:** expression up in disease does not say whether
  inhibition helps, harms, or blocks repair.
- **No targetability filter:** several candidates are intracellular structural,
  chromatin, or homeostatic genes with difficult or unsafe intervention paths.
- **No foundation-model promotion:** the broad residual table is expression
  residual evidence, not named-gene perturbation validation.

## Missing Controls

These controls are required before any broad residual-gate candidate can be
promoted:

- Independent donor-level replication in the intended lead disease and cell
  type, not the same h5ad discovery panel.
- Non-IBD replication for any candidate whose strict support is currently IBD
  stromal.
- Strict compartment-specific analysis with no pooling across stromal,
  epithelial, myeloid, APC, keratinocyte, endocrine, and microglial contexts.
- Residualization against injury, fibrosis/matrix, hypoxia, proliferation,
  apoptosis, mitochondrial stress, epithelial repair, vascular activation,
  myeloid abundance, IFN, TNF/NF-kB, and complement modules.
- Non-autoimmune inflamed controls: infectious colitis, wound healing,
  non-autoimmune dermatitis, fibrosis, ischemic injury, and non-MS
  neuroinflammation.
- Longitudinal severity controls to distinguish causal drivers from late
  damage/repair markers.
- Protein-level validation, especially for secreted/matrix/complement
  candidates.
- Genetic or qTL colocalization where possible, with directionality.
- Perturbation in primary disease-relevant cells, including knockdown and
  overexpression/activation when direction is uncertain.
- Repair and safety counterscreens: barrier integrity, matrix turnover,
  fibroblast contraction, phagocytosis/efferocytosis, host defense, metabolic
  fitness, hypoxia adaptation, and cell viability.
- Predeclared benchmark positives and negatives to calibrate residual-gate
  effect sizes and false discovery.

## Promotion Bar

Before any named candidate is promoted, require all of the following:

1. Same-cell-type replication in an independent dataset for one lead
   indication.
2. Non-IBD support if the claim is cross-autoimmune.
3. Survival after expanded residualization for injury, fibrosis, hypoxia,
   severity, and cell composition.
4. Perturbation evidence showing disease-module normalization, not generic
   toxicity or blocked repair.
5. Directionality evidence proving whether inhibition, activation, or
   stratification is intended.
6. Druggability and safety rationale specific to the target, not a generic
   pathway story.
7. Prior-art review before narrative drafting.

## Bottom Line

The broad residual-gate pivot should be treated as a cautionary negative result.
It shows that a wide screen can still recover coherent-looking but nonspecific
stress, stromal, complement, matrix, hypoxia, and repair genes after stricter
candidate hypotheses fail. The requested candidates are not promoted. The next
valid move is not story polishing; it is a hard replication and perturbation
gate with predeclared no-go rules.
