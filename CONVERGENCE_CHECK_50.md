## Agreement

- The lipid-loader/lysosomal state remains visible across autoimmune tissues
  and treatment-response contexts.
- Direct marker genes do not survive as intervention nodes.
- FABP5 is the strongest local lipid-neighborhood controller signal but is
  directly prior-art blocked for MS/EAE.
- GPR183 is druggable in principle and strongly IBD-response-associated, but
  the current reproducible run closes it on local biology: absent MS receptor
  anchor, absent ligand-production anchor, no coherent ligand/receptor/response
  disease context, and response support in only one system.
- Controller routes split into two groups:
  - autoimmune response routes without MS support (`CD300`, `AHR`, `FPR2`,
    `GPR65`, `GPR183`, `SQLE`);
  - MS lipid-efflux route with prior-art/safety blockade (`LXR_ABCA1_ABCG1`).

## Disagreement

- IBD anti-TNF nonresponse repeatedly points to myeloid/APC trafficking and
  lipid/metabolic routes.
- MS white matter points more toward lipid efflux and lysosomal/APC biology
  than toward the IBD response routes.
- Broad h5ad atlas support is strongest for generic metabolic/lipid state
  markers, not for a selective therapeutic controller.
