- lipid/lysosomal/APC inflammatory myeloid or epithelial-stromal stress states
  recur across several autoimmune tissues,
- IFN/HLA-II/CD74 antigen-presentation readouts are repeatedly coupled to this
  state,
- successful broad anti-inflammatory exposure can weakly reduce some module
  readouts after treatment.

The translational bottleneck is the intervention point, not the existence of
the state.

test controller classes:
