# Newcomer Comprehension Test: Facilitator Handoff

This is the compact operating sheet for the
[full comprehension kit](COMPREHENSION_TEST_KIT.md). It tests documentation,
not the participant, scientific truth, health, or medical suitability. No V55
human test has run yet. `[E01, E03]`

## Before The Session

- Choose exactly one route: `A` two-minute orientation, `B` contributor
  readiness, or `C` idea production.
- Record the onboarding commit and the exact pages supplied.
- Use a volunteer comfortable reading technical English who has ideally not
  read the repository.
- Assign a non-identifying study code only if one is needed.
- Do not collect diagnosis, symptoms, treatment, disability, family history,
  employer-sensitive information, or a real patient scenario.
- Obtain the appropriate ethics/privacy determination before formal research,
  publication, or collection beyond ordinary documentation feedback.
- Open the scorecard but do not pre-fill answers.

## Give Only The Chosen Route

| route | pages | time | items |
|---|---|---:|---|
| A | Two-minute narrative and research map; glossary only if the reader chooses it | up to 5 min | Q01-Q06 |
| B | Fifteen-minute narrative, visual guide, open-problem board, contribution guide | 15-25 min | Q01-Q12 |
| C | Question starters, ten-minute exercise, known non-solutions | 12-15 min | Q13-Q15 plus the produced fictional test card |

Do not expose a longer route and score it as a shorter one. Record every extra
page the reader opens.

## Read This Script Verbatim

> We are testing the documentation, not you. Please use the supplied pages as
> you normally would. Confusion is useful feedback. Do not share personal
> health information. I will not correct or hint during the first pass.

For Route C add:

> Please draft a fictional research-direction test card. We are evaluating
> whether the instructions make the proposal reviewable, not whether your idea
> is biologically correct.

## During The First Pass

- Ask questions exactly as written in the full kit.
- Do not praise, correct, paraphrase, or lead.
- Record the first answer before asking, “What in the material led you there?”
- Record navigation paths, hesitation, unknown terms, and requested pages.
- Never convert the session into medical advice or discuss personal treatment.
- For Route C, do not supply the rival explanation, drop rule, or prior closure
  for the participant.

## Score The Documentation Outcome

| score | meaning |
|---:|---|
| 2 | Clear distinction and scope in the participant's own words. |
| 1 | Main direction is right but one important limit is missing. |
| 0 | Absent or unrelated without a dangerous claim. |
| -1 | Dangerous upgrade to advice, target/cure, progression result, biological absence, or ungrounded authority. |

- Score the first answer, not the corrected answer.
- Do not reward vocabulary repetition without an explained distinction.
- Apply route-specific critical-item and total rules from the full kit.
- Report item patterns; a total must not hide one dangerous overread.
- Never describe the score as intelligence, expertise, or contributor quality.

## Correct Safety-Critical Misunderstandings

After the first pass, immediately correct any belief that the monitoring score
can guide a person's treatment, is a clinical test, is a target/cure, or
measures progression. Show the source-linked boundary. `[M01, M05, P01-P02]`

## Turn A Failure Into A Page Repair

| observed failure | first repair destination |
|---|---|
| Monitor becomes target or treatment choice | `CASE_STUDY_MONITOR_VS_TARGET.md` and the wording on the page actually read |
| Missing progression data becomes absent biology | `CASE_STUDY_PROGRESSION_SNAPSHOT_VS_MOVIE.md` and the first progression statement |
| Outside/model context becomes evidence | `CASE_STUDY_CONTEXT_TO_TEST.md` and `STATUS_DECODER.md` |
| Workflow becomes scientific verdict | `STATUS_DECODER.md` and `REVIEW_RESPONSE_TEMPLATES.md` |
| Idea lacks rival or drop rule | `FIRST_IDEA_IN_TEN_MINUTES.md` |
| Idea repeats a tested shortcut | `KNOWN_NON_SOLUTIONS.md` and the relevant status card |
| Reader cannot find a next action | `README.md`, `COLLABORATOR_ROUTES.md`, or the page where navigation stopped |
| Specialist term blocks meaning | Define it at first use, then update `GLOSSARY.md` if reusable |

Change wording, order, links, or examples. Do not change a scientific claim to
raise a comprehension score. Run the onboarding, source, provenance, and
structural checks after every repair, then retest with a new reader.

## Close The Session

Record:

- date, commit, route, supplied/opened pages, and completion time;
- item scores and dangerous overreads;
- navigation and wording failures;
- page repairs and retest status;
- confirmation that no health information was collected; and
- the sentence: “This was a documentation test, not scientific validation.”

Use the
[TSV scorecard](templates/NEWCOMER_COMPREHENSION_SCORECARD_V55.tsv). Report
aggregate documentation findings unless the participant explicitly agreed to
attribution.
