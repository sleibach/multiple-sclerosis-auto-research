# Human Pilot Recruitment Handoff

This handoff recruits volunteers to test documentation, not MS biology, medical
knowledge, intelligence, or clinical suitability. No V55 human pilot has run.
The invitation below must not be described as a study result. `[E01, E03]`

Use this with the [full comprehension kit](COMPREHENSION_TEST_KIT.md) and the
[facilitator handoff](COMPREHENSION_FACILITATOR_HANDOFF.md). Those documents
control the routes, questions, scoring, and correction procedure.

## Before Recruiting

1. Decide whether the activity is ordinary documentation feedback or formal
   human-subject research under the applicable institution and jurisdiction.
2. Obtain any required ethics, privacy, accessibility, or compensation review
   before collecting responses.
3. Freeze the tested repository commit, one route per participant, session
   length, retention period, and whether recording is permitted.
4. Default to no audio, video, screen, or name recording. Written task outcomes
   are enough for this pilot.
5. Prepare a non-identifying session code and the route-specific scorecard.
6. Name one person who will remove unsafe submissions without copying them.

Do not recruit until these decisions are recorded. Convenience does not justify
collecting health information or changing the route after seeing early results.

## Who To Invite

The intended audience is an adult who reads technical English and has not
already studied this repository. Medical training is not required.

Seek variation in problem-solving background, not health status:

| perspective | useful documentation question |
|---|---|
| Software/data engineering | Can the reader find the runnable boundary and failure conditions? |
| Statistics/measurement | Are uncertainty, confounding, and independent tests distinguishable? |
| Design/human factors | Are status, warnings, and navigation visible without specialist vocabulary? |
| Another scientific field | Can a specialist transfer a method without treating analogy as evidence? |
| General systems thinking | Can the reader frame a coupled problem without upgrading it to a target? |
| Smart generalist | Does the fast route transmit the honest bottom line without prior domain knowledge? |

Do **not** recruit on the basis of diagnosis, symptoms, treatment, disability,
family history, or experience as a patient. Do not ask for those facts.

## Minimal Screening Questions

Ask only what is needed to assign the documentation route:

1. Are you at least 18 and comfortable reading technical English?
2. Have you previously read this repository in depth?
3. Which broad problem-solving background best describes the perspective you
   would bring? “No specialist background” is a valid answer.
4. Can you join a 10-30 minute documentation session without sharing personal,
   private, medical, employer-confidential, or client-confidential material?
5. Do you need an accessibility accommodation for the documentation format?

An accommodation request is operational information. Record only what is
needed to provide it, keep it outside the public repository, and delete it
under the pre-agreed retention rule.

## Copy-Ready Invitation

> We are testing whether public documentation about a computational multiple
> sclerosis research project is understandable to smart readers without a
> medical background. We are testing the pages, not your knowledge or ability.
> The session takes 10-30 minutes and may involve explaining the project's
> current boundary or drafting a fictional research test card. This is not
> medical research, diagnosis, treatment advice, or scientific validation. Do
> not share any health information, personal records, private data, or
> credentials. Participation is voluntary, and confusion is useful feedback.
> Please ask the organizer about privacy, recording, retention, accessibility,
> or compensation before agreeing.

Do not add “help us prove the project works,” “validate the biomarker,” or any
language that implies the expected answer. Recruitment must not prime the live
lead, progression boundary, or desired score.

## Route Assignment

Assign the route before the session. Do not switch a participant to an easier
route after a misunderstanding.

| route | purpose | planned session |
|---|---|---:|
| A | Can the shortest orientation prevent dangerous overreads? | 10-15 min |
| B | Can a reader explain the frontier and locate a contribution path? | 25-35 min |
| C | Can the tools produce a reviewable fictional test card? | 20-25 min |

Use more than one participant per route before describing a recurring
documentation pattern. Do not pool Route A, B, and C totals: exposure and task
differ. A small convenience pilot is diagnostic feedback, not a population
estimate.

## Scheduling Message

Send only:

- date, time zone, and expected duration;
- remote or in-person format;
- the no-recording default or approved recording terms;
- the documentation-only purpose and privacy boundary;
- the accessibility contact route; and
- a statement that the participant may stop without giving a reason.

Do not send the tested pages early unless advance exposure is part of the
frozen route. Do not request a biography, diagnosis, employer, or detailed
medical/scientific credentials.

## Session Packet

The facilitator receives:

1. one non-identifying session code;
2. the exact commit and assigned route;
3. only the pages permitted for that route;
4. the route-specific questions and scorecard;
5. the safety-correction script; and
6. a blank result-capture sheet.

Use a private copy of the
[human pilot session capture template](templates/HUMAN_PILOT_SESSION_CAPTURE_V55.md).
Do not commit a completed individual sheet to the public repository.

The participant receives the invitation, privacy boundary, chosen route, and
stop/withdraw instruction. They do not receive expected answers before their
first response pass.

## What May Be Reported

Allowed after a real pilot:

- route and commit;
- number of completed documentation sessions;
- aggregate item patterns and navigation failures;
- exact page repairs prompted by those failures; and
- confirmation that no health information was collected.

Not allowed:

- participant health or identity details;
- claims about intelligence, expertise, or medical understanding;
- pooling unlike routes into one success rate;
- treating a comprehension score as scientific evidence; or
- saying the monitoring lead, target search, or progression work was validated.

Every report must include: “This was a documentation test, not scientific
validation.”

## Recruitment Readiness Checklist

- [ ] Local ethics/privacy classification recorded.
- [ ] Commit, route, timing, and retention frozen.
- [ ] No-recording default or approved recording terms recorded.
- [ ] Invitation contains the no-health-data and no-medical-advice boundary.
- [ ] Screening collects no health status or detailed identity data.
- [ ] Accessibility route is available.
- [ ] Facilitator has the fixed script and safety correction.
- [ ] Result capture uses a non-identifying code.
- [ ] Report language is bounded to documentation behavior.

Until every applicable item is complete, the pilot remains `not run`.
