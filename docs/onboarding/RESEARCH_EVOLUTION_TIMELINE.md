# How The Research Changed Its Mind

This is a timeline of **evidence transitions**, not a victory sequence. Ideas
entered as candidates, met harder tests, and were retained, bounded, demoted, or
closed. A negative result is therefore part of the main story rather than a
footnote.

V55 adds no scientific result. Every scientific statement below points to an
existing bounded claim and its controlling artifact.

## How To Read The Timeline

Each period has three columns:

1. **Question or candidate:** what seemed worth testing at the time.
2. **Harder test:** the evidence requirement that could disconfirm it.
3. **Current verdict:** what remains after that test, including nulls and data
   boundaries.

Version numbers mark the order of project work. They do not imply that later
work is automatically better or that every version was an independent study.

## V12-V21: Cross-Disease Genetics Became A Direction Problem

**Question.** Could genetic relationships between MS and other autoimmune
diseases reveal a transferable target?

**What survived.** Ulcerative colitis was the strongest genome-wide genetic
comparator among those tested. At ZMIZ1, however, the same expression-increasing
direction was associated with higher MS risk and lower Crohn risk. Together,
these results made “shared disease biology” useful context but unsafe shorthand
for “same intervention.” `[G01-G02]`

**What changed status.** PTGER4 did not survive signal- and direction-specific
review as a simple shared MS-UC target. GPR25 moved from an attractive early
candidate to a demoted route after denser immune-QTL and direction review. The
chr1 KIF21B/GPR25 region remains biologically relevant context, while causal-gene
uncertainty and a difficult restoration/up-function direction keep the
therapeutic route closed. `[G03-G05]`

## V22-V32: One Monitoring Lead Survived, With Narrower Meaning

**Question.** Could an early on-treatment APC/HLA-II expression change monitor
later treatment response?

**What survived.** The rule was locked before the next test. Across its bounded
19-person internal evidence set, the simple score survived multiple statistical
stress tests and outperformed the more flexible alternatives tested there. It
remains the project's one live research lead. `[M01, M03]`

**What narrowed.** Results were mixed across therapies and disease contexts.
The score was not explained by the tested steroid-response or simple
cell-composition signatures, but broader immune tone attenuated it. The honest
description is therefore a **provisional, immune-tone-bounded monitoring
signal**, not a pure APC mechanism, treatment selector, target, or clinical
tool. `[M02, M04-M05]`

Read the source trail:
[locked finding](../findings/FINDING_V22.md),
[robustness map](../workups/treatment_response/ROBUSTNESS_MAP_V28.md), and
[confounder audit](../workups/treatment_response/CONFOUNDER_AUDIT_V32.md).

## V25-V28: More Complexity Did Not Earn More Authority

**Question.** Could a patient-level simulator or a coupled multifeature model
improve on the simple score?

**What survived.** A coupled APC architecture repeatedly linked HLA-II,
IFN/APC, MIF/CD74-related state, IFN readout, and lysosomal processing. That is
supported mechanistic context and useful assay-design input. `[D01]`

**What did not.** The held data could not validate a broad patient-level immune
state simulator. Adding coupled-axis features or flexible multifeature models
did not improve the locked score. The recurring architecture therefore did not
become a successor rule or a multi-node target. `[D02-D03]`

[deep structure](../findings/DEEP_STRUCTURE_V26.md), and

## V33-V40: Broad Exploration Mostly Clarified The Filters

**Question.** Would wider hypotheses, independent model lenses, dormant leads,
or unconventional frames reveal a new intervention route?

**What the program learned.** Ideas generated by models remained proposals, not
evidence. Across the project's documented 20-item failure frame, context or
axis dependence was enriched among cross-axis transfer failures; the apparent
direction/modality pattern remained suggestive rather than established. This
became a practical prefilter, not a universal law of MS. `[E03, F01]`

**Current meaning.** This phase did not replace the monitoring lead or promote
an intervention-grade target. Its durable contribution was stricter question
selection: test context, direction, and transfer before investing in target
work.

[failure-structure analysis](../history/FAILURE_STRUCTURE_AND_EXCLUSION_V39.md)

## V41: Joint Inference Located A Corpus Boundary

**Question.** Could weak signals that were sub-threshold in separate modalities
become credible when all held evidence was modeled together?

**Test.** Candidates had to recur under a formal null and predict structure in
a modality held out from derivation.

**Verdict.** The joint analysis recovered already-known APC and immune-tone
structure, but zero of 22 unexpected candidates passed the combined gate. The
corpus-specific 95% upper bound was `0.127`. That number bounds this search
procedure over this assembled corpus; it is not a biological effect limit or a
claim that all future computation is exhausted. The rational next discovery
input became genuinely new data rather than unconstrained remining of the same
corpus. `[D04-D05]`

Read the [joint-inference record](../history/JOINT_INFERENCE_V41.md).

## V42-V46: Preparation Improved Without Pretending To Be Biology

**Question.** Could the external monitoring test be made mechanical enough that
the result cannot be rescued or reinterpreted after the data arrive?

**What changed.** The rule, ingestion behavior, analysis count, confounder
reporting, and pass/fail/inconclusive interpretation were frozen. Seeded
synthetic cohorts then tested power and software behavior, including difficult
batch conditions. `[A01, A03]`

**What did not change.** Simulations and harness checks say how the method
behaves under stated assumptions. They provide no new biological evidence and
do not increase the monitoring lead's evidence grade. The audited public-data
search still found no fresh ready-to-run primary cohort; Gafson remained an
access request and Karolinska still needed response-label mapping. `[A03-A04]`

## V47-V52: Broader Context And Structure Did Not Reopen Closed Targets

**Question.** Could outside knowledge, restored genetics access, or predicted
protein structure rehabilitate direction-closed therapeutic routes?

**Boundary first.** Outside-source knowledge was segregated from rerunnable
project evidence. Predicted structures remained prediction context with
confidence attached, not experimental proof. `[E01-E02]`

**Verdict.** Structural tractability could inform assay or modality design, but
it could not supply a missing causal gene, protective direction, relevant-cell
effect, or functional rescue. KIF21B/GPR25 and PTGER4 remained closed as target
routes. The defensible near-term impact remained external validation of the
monitoring lead, not a structure-rescued target. `[G03-G05, M05, A01]`

Read the [therapeutic-path synthesis](../reports/THERAPEUTIC_PATH_V52.md) and
[evidence-class policy](../knowledge/EPISTEMIC_CLASSES.md).

## V53-V54: Progression Work Exposed The Design Wall

**Question.** Could held lesion, microglia, stage, or treatment data reveal a
molecular state that predicts disability progression or a direction-resolved
progression target?

**Corrections that mattered.** Source and diagnosis were strongly entangled in
one microglia partition, showing how acquisition source can mimic disease. A
foamy OXPHOS/lysosomal morphology pattern was downgraded after global
multiplicity and within-donor checks. `[C01-C02, P04]`

**What remains.** The exact CD44/CXCR4 microglia state is retained only as an
identity-fixed, data-gated candidate; progression prediction is untested. None
of the audited packages contains the required longitudinal molecular-to-
confirmed-disability design, and no progression marker, mechanism, target,
treatment effect, or halt strategy was established. `[P01-P06]`

## V55: Make The Frontier Legible Without Moving It

V55 changes communication, not science. It makes the live lead, closed routes,
nulls, confounding lessons, and missing-data requirements easier to inspect. A
newcomer should leave with two open evidence-bearing moves:

1. run the frozen monitoring rule on a correctly structured independent
   cohort; and
2. acquire a microglia-compatible longitudinal molecular-to-confirmed-
   disability package for progression. `[A01-A02]`

The first can validate, narrow, or close a monitoring association. The second
can begin a progression test. Neither outcome is guaranteed, and neither is a
shortcut to a treatment target.

## The Shortest Honest Summary

The program did not march from target to cure. It repeatedly changed its mind:

- broad cross-disease target transfer became direction-specific context;
- an early genetics favorite was demoted;
- one treatment-response score survived but acquired tighter boundaries;
- systems complexity supplied context but not better prediction;
- joint analysis bounded further mining of the same corpus;
- progression candidates weakened under source, donor, and multiplicity
  controls; and
- the next credible advances now depend on specifically designed independent
  data.

That pattern is the point. The repository's useful output is not only what
survived, but a traceable record of why attractive alternatives did not.
