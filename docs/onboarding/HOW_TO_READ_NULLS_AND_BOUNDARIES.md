# How To Read Nulls, Closures, And Missing Answers

Research does not produce only “yes” and “no.” This repository uses several
different non-positive outcomes because they imply different decisions. Merging
them would make the project look simpler and make its conclusions less true.

This page explains existing statuses. It creates no new scientific claim.

## The Seven Outcomes At A Glance

| outcome | plain meaning | what it permits | what it forbids |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Negative established** | A fair, scoped test did not support the proposed improvement or claim. | Stop spending on that route under the tested framing. | “The entire biology is irrelevant.” |
| **Closed or demoted** | A route lacks a required causal, directional, or evidentiary link after direct review. | Reopen only with the named missing evidence. | Repackaging the same ambiguity as a fresh lead. |
| **Mixed** | A rule or relationship worked in some eligible contexts and not others. | Narrow the scope and test transport explicitly. | Universal generalization or selective reporting of passes. |
| **Attenuated** | Adjustment for a competing explanation weakens the association. | Retain a bounded interpretation and report both raw and adjusted results. | Calling the original story pure or confounder-free. |
| **Inconclusive** | The data cannot distinguish the pre-specified pass and fail interpretations with enough precision. | Report effect and uncertainty; use them to design a later study. | Calling it a pass, a kill, or permission to retune. |
| **Unscoreable or invalid** | Required inputs, mappings, timing, permissions, or process checks are missing or violated. | Repair the package or record that it cannot answer the question. | Reporting a biological null. |
| **Data blocked** | The required study design does not exist in the held corpus. | Specify and acquire the missing design. | “The mechanism does not exist.” |

## 1. Negative Established: A Scoped “No”

A negative-established result follows a fair test of a defined claim. It is
valuable because it removes a tempting option from the decision set.

Project examples:

- Adding coupled-axis features or flexible multifeature models did not improve
  the locked monitoring score in the held small data. This closes those tested
  successor formulations; it does not prove simple models always beat complex
  ones on future data. `[D02]`
- The held data could not validate the broad patient-level simulator for
  patient prediction or unseen-pathway simulation. Bounded directional priors
  may still be useful; the unsupported authority is the broad simulator claim.
  `[D03]`
- The foamy OXPHOS/lysosomal morphology pattern weakened under complete
  multiplicity and within-donor checks. It remains exploratory morphology
  context, not a progression mechanism or target. `[P04]`

**Decision:** stop treating the tested route as if it survived. Preserve the
bounded residue and state what genuinely different evidence could change the
answer.

## 2. Closed Or Demoted: The Route Failed, Not Necessarily The Region

A closed lead can still point to real biology. What closed is the chain from
association to an intervention in the required direction.

At the chr1 KIF21B/GPR25 region, the regional biology remained plausible while
causal-gene uncertainty and restoration/up-function direction kept the target
route closed. GPR25 itself was demoted after denser immune-QTL and direction
review. PTGER4 closed as a simple shared MS-UC target because signals and
disease directions conflicted. `[G03-G05]`

Those statements do **not** mean:

- the chr1 region has no relationship to MS;
- GPR25 was experimentally proven irrelevant;
- PTGER4 has no biology in any disease; or
- a predicted pocket or familiar protein family can reopen the route.

They mean that target nomination requires a signal-specific causal gene, an
aligned protective direction in the relevant cell/state, and a modality that
can deliver that direction. Repeating regional association or tractability
precedent does not supply those links.

**Decision:** keep the route closed until the named chain changes.

## 3. Mixed: Scope Is The Result

The locked APC/HLA-II score did not behave uniformly across every tested
therapy or disease context. The bounded internal set remained promising, while
the unbounded transfer claim did not. `[M01-M03]`

The correct response to mixed transport is not to average away the failures or
choose only the passes. It is to state:

- which contexts were eligible;
- where the rule passed and failed;
- which module implementation was exact or approximate;
- what common context defines the remaining claim; and
- what outside cohort can test that scope without retuning.

**Decision:** retain a narrower provisional monitor, not a universal rule.

## 4. Attenuated: A Competing Explanation Matters

The monitoring score was not explained by the tested steroid-response or
simple marker-level cell-composition signatures, but broader metabolic,
inflammatory, and STAT1/immune-tone adjustment attenuated it. Direct steroid
exposure metadata were not measured. `[M04]`

Attenuation is neither “nothing changed” nor “artifact proven.” It means the
specific APC/HLA-II interpretation cannot be separated cleanly from a broader
immune-state shift in these data.

**Decision:** report raw and adjusted results together; call the monitor
immune-tone bounded; do not upgrade it to a pure mechanism.

## 5. Inconclusive: Uncertainty Crosses The Decision Boundary

An inconclusive validation does not satisfy the pre-specified pass or fail
criteria. With a small cohort, the estimated effect may point in a direction
while its interval remains compatible with materially different answers.

The V42 interpretation grid fixes this before outside data are seen. Seeded
method simulations indicate that a very small cohort may remain inconclusive
unless the true separation is large and labels are clean. Those simulations
describe study behavior under assumptions, not the expected MS effect. `[A01,
A03]`

An inconclusive result can still provide:

- an effect-size estimate;
- a confidence interval;
- information about label quality and scoreability;
- a power input for a later cohort; and
- evidence about whether the observed direction is compatible across cohorts.

It cannot provide:

- a hidden pass;
- a reason to move the threshold;
- a post-hoc subgroup success; or
- a kill when the pre-specified fail criterion was not met.

**Decision:** preserve uncertainty and use it to size the next fair test.

## 6. Unscoreable Or Invalid: No Biological Verdict

A package may contain expression data and still be unable to test the rule.
Examples include missing person-level pairing, no compatible response label,
ineligible timing, insufficient frozen-module coverage, contradictory metadata,
or use terms that do not permit analysis.

The outcome is then **unscoreable** or **invalid**, not negative. A missing gene
set is not a score of zero. A blocked license is not a failed biomarker. A late
sample is not an early-treatment null. `[A01, A04]`

**Decision:** repair the package if possible; otherwise record that the question
was not answered.

## 7. Data Blocked: The Required Experiment Is Absent

Progression asks whether a molecular state measured first predicts later,
confirmed disability accumulation. The held corpus does not contain that
linked longitudinal design. Cross-sectional disease-stage labels, one-time
disability values, postmortem lesion morphology, and pharmacodynamic response
cannot substitute. `[P01, P03, P05]`

This supports two statements at once:

1. No progression marker, transition, mechanism, target, treatment effect, or
   halt strategy was established under the available analyses. `[P02]`
2. The project has not shown that progression biology is absent or that a
   molecular state could never predict it.

**Decision:** acquire repeated molecular and confirmed-disability measurements
with compatible time, treatment, relapse, source, and quality provenance.

## Corpus Boundary: A Search Result, Not A Law Of Nature

V41 asked whether weak signals could become credible when combined across all
held modalities. Zero of 22 unexpected candidates passed the recurrence-plus-
held-out gate, yielding a corpus-specific 95% upper bound of `0.127`. `[D04]`

That result says more unconstrained mining of the same assembled corpus is not
the rational discovery move under that gate. It does not say:

- all public data are exhausted forever;
- no future method can help;
- no biological effect larger than `0.127` exists; or
- targeted validation and genuinely new data should stop. `[D05]`

**Decision:** require new people, time, modality, outcome, perturbation, or a
precommitted held-out role rather than another flexible pass over the same
labels.

## A Four-Question Interpretation Flow

### 1. Was The Test Eligible And Valid?

If required data, permission, mappings, timing, or process checks fail, report
**unscoreable/invalid**. Stop before biological interpretation.

### 2. Was The Exact Claim Actually Tested?

If the design cannot identify the estimand, report **data blocked**. A proxy may
answer a different question; it cannot inherit the intended claim.

### 3. Was There Enough Information For The Pre-Specified Decision?

If uncertainty spans pass and fail regions, report **inconclusive** with effect
and interval.

### 4. What Did The Fair Test Show?

- If a proposed improvement or transfer fails its gate, record a scoped
  **negative** or **closure**.
- If performance differs by context, report **mixed** and narrow the scope.
- If adjustment weakens specificity, report **attenuated** and retain the
  confounder boundary.
- If the pre-specified pass criterion is met, report only the level the design
  supports; a monitoring pass still is not target or clinical-utility evidence.

## Language That Preserves The Answer

| avoid | use instead |
|---|---|
| “No effect.” | “The pre-specified test did not support an effect of the stated size/scope,” if that is what the interval and power support. |
| “The biomarker failed.” | “The package was unscoreable because pairing/labels/coverage were missing,” when no valid test ran. |
| “There is no progression signal.” | “No progression signal was identifiable in the held designs.” |
| “The target is dead.” | “The target route remains closed on causal gene and therapeutic direction.” |
| “It almost passed.” | “The result was inconclusive under the pre-specified rule.” |
| “Adjustment removed it, so it was fake.” | “The association attenuated under the named competing explanation.” |
| “The model found nothing.” | “No unexpected candidate passed the specified recurrence-plus-holdout gate.” |

## Why This Matters For Contributors

A useful idea includes its non-positive outcomes before analysis:

- What makes the input invalid or unscoreable?
- What result is a clean negative?
- What interval is inconclusive?
- What finding narrows rather than kills the claim?
- Which missing design blocks the question?
- What exact evidence would reopen a closure?

That structure prevents every result from being narrated as encouragement. It
also makes honest nulls useful: each one tells the next contributor whether to
stop, repair, narrow, acquire data, or test a genuinely different route.
