# MS Research Frontier: One-Page Collaborator Brief

**Audience:** smart people outside medicine who may bring a useful method,
system reframe, data route, or failure-detection idea.

**Status:** communication only. This brief adds no scientific claim.

[Open the print-designed HTML version](COLLABORATOR_BRIEF_V55.html).

## The Problem In One Paragraph

Multiple sclerosis involves immune-mediated injury in the central nervous
system. Relapses are episodes of inflammatory activity; progression asks
whether disability accumulates and remains confirmed over time. They are
related but not interchangeable. Halting progression is the north-star problem,
and this project has not established a progression mechanism, target, treatment
effect, or halt strategy. `[B01-B02, P01-P02]`

## The Honest Status Strip

| status | current answer |

| **Supported context** | A coupled APC architecture recurs, and genetics provides MS-UC context plus a ZMIZ1 direction warning. None is an intervention-grade target. `[D01, G01-G02]` |
| **Closed or negative** | KIF21B/GPR25 and PTGER4 target routes remain direction/causal-signal closed; added complexity and a broad simulator did not earn predictive authority. `[G03-G05, D02-D03]` |
| **Corpus boundary** | Joint inference returned known structure but zero of 22 unexpected candidates passed the recurrence-plus-holdout gate. More unconstrained mining of the same corpus is not the rational discovery move. `[D04-D05]` |
| **Progression boundary** | The held corpus lacks repeated molecular measurements linked to later confirmed disability. CD44/CXCR4 is identity-only for a future compatible test. `[P01-P06]` |

## What Was Learned About Failure

- A shared disease or locus does not determine a shared therapeutic direction.
- A tractable protein shape cannot supply a missing causal gene or protective
  direction.
- A more flexible model can fit the same small labels without transporting.
- Source, batch, donor, composition, and broad immune tone can imitate a
  specific biological story.
- Missing longitudinal design is not evidence that progression biology is
  absent. `[F01, C02, G02-G05, M03-M04, P01-P05]`

## Six Puzzles Where An Outsider Could Help

1. **Small-cohort inference:** make a frozen pass/fail/inconclusive test
   maximally informative without moving its threshold.
2. **Access and substitution:** locate a legally usable paired response cohort,
   or prove precisely why a near-match cannot answer the same question.
3. **The progression movie:** design or locate repeated molecular state before
   repeated confirmed-disability outcomes in the same people.
4. **Restoration-direction intervention:** propose a modality that can increase
   or restore function in the relevant cell/state, rather than defaulting to
   inhibition.
5. **Coupled-system control:** discriminate a controller from a readout using
   direction-resolved perturbations and competing causal graphs.
6. **Confound detection:** detect source/label entanglement before interpreting
   an apparently disease-specific signal. `[A01-A04, C02, D01-D02, G03, P01-P05]`

## A Useful Idea Has Seven Parts

1. One bounded question.
2. One directional prediction.
3. A reachable data source and eligibility rule.
4. A comparator, null, or holdout.
5. Confound and multiplicity controls.
6. A result that would make you drop or narrow the idea.
7. The project decision that would change.

An idea is welcome before it is evidence. It becomes a research direction only
when the project can test it fairly. Model confidence, literature enthusiasm,
and outside consensus do not replace that test. `[E01-E03]`

## Do Not Re-Propose These Shortcuts

- “The monitor is the target.”
- “A static stage label measures progression.”
- “A predicted pocket makes the genetics actionable.”
- “A shared autoimmune association implies one intervention.”
- “A new learner on the same tiny labels is independent validation.”
- “No compatible data means the biology is absent.”
