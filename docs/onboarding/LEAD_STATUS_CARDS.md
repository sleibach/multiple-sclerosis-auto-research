# Lead And Frontier Status Cards

These cards are compact orientation, not substitutes for the linked evidence.
Status words are part of each claim.

## Card 1 · Bounded APC/HLA-II Early Monitoring

**Status:** `LIVE · PROVISIONAL · OUTSIDE VALIDATION PENDING`  
**Claim IDs:** `[M01-M05, A01, A03]`

**What it says:** A fixed early-treatment change score has bounded internal
response-monitoring support in a small evidence set. It survived several tool
checks and remained simpler and more stable than tested flexible alternatives.

**What it does not say:** It is not a clinical biomarker, baseline selector,
drug target, treatment recommendation, efficacy measure, or progression result.
Broader immune tone attenuates it.

**What changes status:** A mechanically executed, preregistered independent
cohort test. Pass, fail, inconclusive, and unscoreable outcomes are all allowed.

**Start here:** [Finding V22](../findings/FINDING_V22.md),
[Robustness V28](../workups/treatment_response/ROBUSTNESS_MAP_V28.md),
[Confounder Audit V32](../workups/treatment_response/CONFOUNDER_AUDIT_V32.md),
[Preregistration V42](../validation/PREREGISTRATION_V42.md).

## Card 2 · Coupled APC Immune Architecture

**Status:** `SUPPORTED CONTEXT · NOT A TARGET · NOT A SUCCESSOR RULE`  
**Claim IDs:** `[D01, D02]`

**What it says:** HLA-II, IFN/APC, MIF/CD74-related state, IFN readout, and
lysosomal processing recur as a coupled architecture across held analyses.

**What it does not say:** Co-movement is not a causal network, control map,
multi-node therapy, or improved predictor. Added coupled features did not beat
the locked scalar.

**What changes status:** A prospective perturbation that distinguishes drivers,
readouts, feedback, and redundancy, followed by held-out functional evidence.

**Start here:** [Deep Structure V26](../findings/DEEP_STRUCTURE_V26.md) and
[Robustness V28](../workups/treatment_response/ROBUSTNESS_MAP_V28.md).

## Card 3 · MS–Ulcerative Colitis Genetic Backdrop

**Status:** `ROBUST CONTEXT · LOW NOVELTY · NO TARGET TRANSFER`  
**Claim IDs:** `[G01]`

**What it says:** Ulcerative colitis had the strongest genome-wide relationship
with MS among the autoimmune comparators tested here.

**What it does not say:** UC causes MS, a UC therapy transfers to MS, or a shared
locus has the same causal gene and therapeutic direction.

**What changes status:** Nothing is queued to “promote” this broad backdrop. A
target claim would require its own signal-, cell-, gene-, and direction-specific
evidence.

**Start here:** [Findings Report V37](../reports/FINDINGS_REPORT_V37.md).

## Card 4 · ZMIZ1 Direction Decoupling

**Status:** `SUPPORTED DECOUPLING · TRANSFER WARNING · NOT A TARGET`  
**Claim IDs:** `[G02]`

**What it says:** An expression-increasing direction aligned with higher MS risk
but lower Crohn risk at ZMIZ1 in the project analysis.

**What it does not say:** ZMIZ1 is actionable, one disease direction is correct
for all cells, or shared-autoimmune genetics provides a shared intervention.

**What changes status:** Signal- and cell-specific causal direction in MS plus a
direction-matched functional test.

**Start here:** [Genetics/eQTL Workup V16](../workups/genetics/GENETICS_EQTL_WORKUP_V16.md).

## Card 5 · chr1 KIF21B/GPR25 And PTGER4 Routes

**Status:** `CLOSED ON DIRECTION OR EVIDENCE`  
**Claim IDs:** `[G03-G05]`

**What it says:** The chr1 region remains biologically real but has unresolved
causal-gene and hard up-function/restoration constraints. GPR25 was demoted.
PTGER4 has multiple signals and conflicting disease directions.

**What it does not say:** These genes are biologically irrelevant. It says they
are not defensible actionable targets under current evidence. Protein class,
known ligands, or predicted structure do not reverse the verdict.

**What changes status:** Signal-specific causal-gene resolution and a feasible
modality with the required sign in the relevant cell/state. Until then, do not
spend as though the route were active.

**Start here:** [chr1 Reevaluation V19](../workups/genetics/GENETICS_CHR1_REEVALUATION_V19.md)
and [Therapeutic Path V52](../reports/THERAPEUTIC_PATH_V52.md).

## Card 6 · Exact CD44/CXCR4 Microglia State

**Status:** `LIVE · IDENTITY ONLY · PROGRESSION DATA GATED`  
**Claim IDs:** `[C01, C02, P06, A02]`

**What it says:** A bounded CD44/CXCR4-high microglial state reproduced in
quality-qualified MS analyses. Its exact two-gene score identity is fixed for a
future compatible progression cohort.

**What it does not say:** It is not progression-specific, causal, a target, or
validated to predict disability. One discovery partition was strongly
brain-bank sensitive. No PBMC, whole-blood, gene, or threshold proxy is allowed.

**What changes status:** Source-balanced donor replication for state identity
and, separately, a longitudinal microglia-compatible test with repeated
confirmed-disability outcomes.

**Start here:** [V53 Run Summary](../history/V53_RUN_SUMMARY.md),
[Source-Balance Addendum](../validation/MS_MICROGLIA_SOURCE_BALANCE_ADDENDUM_V53.md),
[P1 Candidate Handoff](../validation/PROGRESSION_P1_CANDIDATE_STATE_HANDOFF_V54.md).

## Card 7 · Progression Frontier

**Status:** `DATA BLOCKED · NO PROGRESSION RESULT ESTABLISHED`  
**Claim IDs:** `[P01-P05, A02]`

**What it says:** The held corpus has no usable longitudinal
molecular-to-confirmed-disability package. Relapsing-to-progressive transition
was not identifiable in seven audited datasets. No tested module became a
portable stage marker or direction-resolved progression target.

**What it does not say:** Progression biology is absent or molecular prediction
is impossible. The result is a coverage and evidence boundary.

**What changes status:** A package with repeated compatible molecular state,
repeated confirmed disability, timing, treatment, attendance/censoring, source,
site, and quality provenance.

**Start here:** [Progression Frontier V54](../history/PROGRESSION_FRONTIER_V54.md)
and [Cohort Acquisition Spec](../validation/PROGRESSION_COHORT_ACQUISITION_SPEC_V54.md).

## Card 8 · Same-Corpus Discovery Boundary

**Status:** `GROUNDED NULL · CORPUS-SPECIFIC EXHAUSTION`  
**Claim IDs:** `[D04, D05]`

**What it says:** Joint inference recovered known APC/immune-tone structure, but
none of 22 unexpected candidates passed recurrence plus held-out validation.
The corpus-specific upper bound under that gate was 0.127.

**What it does not say:** All public data, methods, or future computation are
exhausted, or 0.127 is a biological effect-size ceiling.

**What changes status:** Genuinely new data or a prospectively fixed targeted
test. Another flexible search on the same assembled labels is not the next
discovery step.

**Start here:** [Joint Inference V41](../history/JOINT_INFERENCE_V41.md).

## Card 9 · Broad Patient-Level Immune Simulator

**Status:** `NEGATIVE ESTABLISHED · NOT VALIDATED FOR PATIENT PREDICTION`  
**Claim IDs:** `[D03]`

**What it says:** The held data did not validate a broad simulator for
individual prediction or unseen-pathway simulation.

**What it does not say:** Every model, directional prior, or bounded simulator
is useless. It closes the larger claim that this implementation can simulate
patient response beyond observed pathways.

**What changes status:** A separately specified model with prospective or
held-out individual-level validation. No rescue by relabeling descriptive
directional scores as simulation.

**Start here:** [Model Card V25](../workups/treatment_response/MODEL_CARD_V25.md).
