# Anti-Duplication Route Review V55

This dated communication audit checks whether every collaborator puzzle makes
three things findable before submission: what not to repeat, the current
project status, and a concrete contribution action. It creates no scientific
claim and does not reopen any closed route. `[E01]`

## Route Contract

Each numbered problem must provide:

1. a scoped **known non-solution** or canonical stop-list route;
2. a **current status** statement with claim IDs and controlling artifacts;
3. a **contribution action** that asks for a test, data package, challenge, or
   communication artifact; and
4. wording that distinguishes “closed in this form” from “universally absent.”

All eight problem sections also retain their own **What is known**, **Already

top of the board is therefore a shortcut into complete context, not a
replacement for it.

## What This Prevents

- A familiar gene, model, or dataset does not re-enter merely under a new
  label.
- A closed target route is not misread as proof that its broader biology is
  absent.
- A proposed repair names the assumption or missing evidence it changes.
- A contributor reaches an action guide suited to the proposed artifact
  instead of submitting an untestable conclusion.

## Limits

- Navigation cannot determine whether a new idea is actually novel.
- A contributor can still misunderstand a scoped closure; human review remains
  necessary.
- A route to a contribution guide does not make the proposal valid or provide
  evidence.
- The unrun human comprehension pilot remains the direct test of whether real
  newcomers use these routes correctly.
