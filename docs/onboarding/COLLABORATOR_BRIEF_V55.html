The problem

MS involves immune-mediated injury in the central nervous system. A relapse is an inflammatory episode; progression asks whether disability accumulates and remains confirmed. They are related, not interchangeable. Halting progression is the north star. [B01-B02]

What survived

  • Monitoring: one fixed early APC/HLA-II change score remains live, small-n, mixed across contexts, and immune-tone bounded. It is not a selector, target, clinical test, or cure. [M01-M05]
  • Context: coupled APC architecture recurs; MS-UC genetics and ZMIZ1 supply transfer/direction warnings, not targets. [D01, G01-G02]
  • Readiness: the outside test is preregistered to report pass, fail, or inconclusive without refitting. [A01, A03]

What closed or hit a boundary

  • KIF21B/GPR25 and PTGER4: causal-signal or therapeutic-direction route closed. [G03-G05]
  • More features and a broad simulator: no earned predictive improvement. [D02-D03]
  • Joint search: known structure returned; 0/22 unexpected candidates passed. [D04-D05]
  • Progression: held data lack repeated molecular state linked to later confirmed disability. CD44/CXCR4 is identity-only. [P01-P06]

Failure lessons

Shared biology does not fix therapeutic direction. A predicted pocket cannot fill a missing causal link. Flexible models do not create independent evidence. Source, donor, batch, composition, and immune tone can imitate specificity. Missing design is not absent biology. [C02, F01]

Six puzzles where outsiders can help

  1. Small-cohort inference: make a frozen test informative without moving its threshold.
  2. Access: find a lawful paired-response cohort, or document why a near-match cannot substitute.
  3. Progression movie: repeated molecular state before repeated confirmed disability in the same people.
  4. Restoration: devise a modality for increasing or restoring function in the relevant cell/state.
  5. Coupled control: distinguish controller from readout with competing causal graphs and directional perturbations.
  6. Confounds: detect source/label entanglement before interpretation. [A01-A04, C02, D01-D02, G03, P01-P05]

A useful idea has seven parts

  1. Bounded question
  2. Directional prediction
  3. Reachable data + eligibility
  4. Comparator/null/holdout
  5. Confound + multiplicity plan
  6. Drop or narrowing rule
  7. Decision it would change

Ideas are welcome before they are evidence. Model or literature confidence cannot replace a fair test. [E01-E03]

Do not re-propose these shortcuts

  • “The monitor is the target.”
  • “A static stage label measures progression.”
  • “A pocket makes the genetics actionable.”
  • “Shared autoimmune association means one intervention.”
  • “A new learner on the same labels is validation.”
  • “No compatible data means no biology.”
