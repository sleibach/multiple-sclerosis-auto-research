# V54 Progression Cohort Candidate Role Matrix

Status: metadata-only audit. No expression values, molecular scores, or
quarantined packages were read.

## Result

Ten known progression-adjacent or validation-route candidates were checked
against the frozen P1 longitudinal progression, P2 compartment localization,
and P3 functional direction contracts. **Zero currently qualifies for any of
the three roles.** This is an acquisition boundary, not a biological null.

The machine-readable matrix is
`analysis/v54_progression_candidate_role_matrix/candidate_role_matrix.tsv` and
is generated by `scripts/v54_progression_candidate_role_matrix.py` from
committed metadata artifacts.

## Decision-Relevant Distinctions

- **Gafson and Karolinska remain valuable monitoring-validation routes.** Their
  requested NEDA-4 or beneficial-response labels do not become disability
  progression endpoints. They cannot satisfy P1 unless raw repeated disability
  components and a documented CDP/PIRA protocol also exist and pass the blind
  gates.
- **GSE228330 is not a progression cohort now.** It lacks a verified subject
  map, repeated disability, relapse/steroid context, measured composition, and
  batch fields. The prepared 29-field addendum is the only route to reassess
  eligibility; current use remains pharmacodynamic context.
- **GSE24427 is the nearest held longitudinal transcriptomic design, but not a
  progression design.** It has repeated expression and a verified map, yet EDSS
  is baseline-only and two-year relapse is not disability accumulation.
- **Postmortem brain and lesion cohorts provide pathology/stage context only.**
  Same-death lesions and PPMS/SPMS cross-sections cannot substitute for a
  living-person trajectory or event-time endpoint.
- **P2 has no candidate.** No package combines the same progression phenotype,
  paired CNS/CSF and blood, verified timing, and eligible composition
  measurement.
- **P3 is correctly empty.** No molecular state first passed a
  progression-specific association gate, so no perturbation or AlphaFold
  context is eligible to promote a target.

## Required Acquisition Archetype

The shortest path is not another public cross-sectional expression dataset. It
is a living-person longitudinal cohort with:

1. molecular baseline plus repeat sampling;
2. baseline and at least two follow-up disability assessments with raw EDSS and
   available T25FW/9HPT components;
3. documented CDP/PIRA adjudication plus relapse, steroid, treatment, and
   censoring dates;
4. direct blood composition measurement;
5. preferably paired CSF and blood for P2;
6. enough events to rerun the blinded binary/event-time and P2 power designs.

The exact field list remains
`docs/validation/PROGRESSION_COHORT_ACQUISITION_SPEC_V54.md`. Until such a
package arrives, progression-target inference is blocked by data identity, not
by lack of another computational method.

## Boundary

No candidate is counted usable from a title, abstract, nominal timepoint, or
derived label. `eligible_metadata_only`, if it ever appears, would still mean
ready for blind intake checks, not analysis success or biological evidence.
