# Convergence Check 5

Timestamp: 2026-05-27 06:24 UTC

## Current Consensus

The V3 run has converged on a negative therapeutic interpretation of the
original lipid-lysosomal/APC/HLA-II module:

- The module is reproducible across local autoimmune single-cell/spatial
  contrasts.
- The strongest recurrent genes and states are mostly IFN/APC/HLA-II,
  lysosomal antigen-processing, myeloid density, tissue injury, and repair
  readouts.
- Under hostile-critique gates, the module does not yet contain a selective,
  correct-direction, druggable, perturbation-supported, and novel intervention
  point.

## Track Returns

### Wave19-A: Tolerogenic Checkpoints

No checkpoint controller promoted.

- `CD274`, `CD24`, `BTLA`, `CD200`, and `CD47` are parked comparator axes only.
- Main failures: missing state coupling, direction mismatch, weak perturbation
  support, broad immune suppression risk, and prior-art saturation.

### Wave19-B: Lysosomal/Lipid Controllers

No upstream lysosomal or lipid-handling controller promoted.

- `LIPA/LAL_enhancement` is parked.
- `NPC1/NPC2` are readouts.
- `LRRK2` is disease-specific/IBD-skewed.
- TFEB/TFE3, TRPML1, PIKFYVE, PPAR/LXR/ABCA1/ABCG1, GBA/GBA2, and generic
  mTOR/autophagy routes are no-go.

### Wave20-A: Unrestricted Survivors

No unrestricted survivor promoted.

- `SNX10` is the least-bad fail-fast comparator, not a target.
- `C15ORF48/MOCCI` is a marker-like inflammation-resolution/autophagy note;
  the local C15ORF48-up/NDUFA4-down switch is not broad.
- `DAP`, `FMNL2`, `NCK1`, `TNFAIP8L1`, `PPIL3`, `PLEK2`, `SEL1L3`, `AQR`, and
  adjacent `CBX3/CHI3L1/LTA4H/PPP3CA/CXCL9` fail residual, modality, safety, or
  prior-art gates.

### Wave20-B: Genetics/Druggable Alternate Axis

No alternate genetically anchored and druggable axis promoted.

- `PTPN2`, `SH2B3`, `CLEC16A`, `ATG16L1`, `OSMR`, `GPR65`, `IRF5`, `CARD9`,
  `IL10`, `TNFAIP3`, `IL6R`, and `TYK2` all fail at least one hard gate.
- The failure is not absence of autoimmune genetics. It is absence of a
  target-level, correct-direction, druggable, perturbation-supported, novel
  intervention package.

## Agreement

All tracks agree that expression recurrence alone is insufficient. The
remaining plausible space is not another rank of APC/HLA-II/IFN markers. A
candidate must survive strict residual specificity and have an actionable
modality before it deserves literature/patent novelty work.

## Disagreement Or Open Space

The current local tables still contain residual-positive, lower-prioritization
genes that were not deeply audited because they were outside the initial module
or because the druggability inventory covered only selected candidates. Examples
include `ATOX1`, `SQLE`, `LDLRAD3`, `IL15`, `CD82`, and several proteasome or
IFN-stress genes.

This is the next forcing question:

> Does a strict-residual, externally druggable, non-APC-marker candidate exist
> outside the exhausted hand-curated candidate lists?

## Next Action

Launch Wave21:

- programmatic broad scan of strict residual candidates against local
  recurrence, MS anchor, ChEMBL/UniProt/OpenTargets evidence, and prior V3
  exclusions;
- focused review of any candidate with at least five autoimmune-disease breadth
  or a strong lead-indication rationale plus plausible modality;
- immediate demotion of generic IFN/JAK/proteasome/core-machinery hits unless a
