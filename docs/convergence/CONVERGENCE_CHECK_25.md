## Agreement

- Direct perturbation evidence can reveal directionality that expression
  recurrence alone misses.
- SLAMF7 is biologically real but not a V3 therapeutic target:
  - stimulation is generic-inflammatory;
  - antagonist/signaling-bias intervention is untested;
  - existing clinical antibody precedent is not a clean antagonist;
  - MS direction is conflicted;
  - repair and host-defense guardrails are missing.

## Disagreement

- Genetics is broad for SLAMF7, but local disease-cell state evidence is narrow.
- Direct perturbation shows both inflammatory amplification and suppression of
  some APC/lipid modules. That mixed output is not a clean therapeutic
  direction.

## Decision

Close SLAMF7 as assay-design material.
