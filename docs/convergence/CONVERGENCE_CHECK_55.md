# Convergence Check 55

Timestamp: 2026-05-27 21:08 CEST.

## Forcing Question

After `LITAF` and `CASP4` failed as promotable upstream stress generators, can
an endogenous inflammasome/caspase brake provide a tractable intervention point
for the cross-autoimmune C15ORF48/MOCCI branch?

## Evidence Integrated

- Wave99 upstream stress-generator audit:
  - `LITAF` and `CASP4` remain biologically useful ordering hypotheses.
  - Neither passed MS anchoring, target-resolved genetics, direct perturbation,
    and modality/prior-art gates.
- Wave99B local brake audit:
  - Tested `17` endogenous brake and pyroptosis comparator genes.
  - Reopened therapeutic targets: `0`.
  - Top candidate `CARD16` was recurrent across `5` diseases and C15-adjacent
    across `4` diseases, but residual C15 case-positive disease count was only
    `1`, MS p was `0.4948`, and no perturbation/genetics/modality gate passed.
  - `SERPINB1` had cleaner brake biology but weak local MS/C15 support.
  - `IL18BP` had the cleanest intervention-like modality but weak local support
    and heavy IL18-axis prior art.
- Directionality sidecar:
  - Disease-high `CARD16` is not interpretable as augmentation evidence.
  - `SERPINB1` is a clean biology comparator, not a local V3 target.
- Prior-art/druggability sidecar:
  - No GO. `CARD16` is only a local ordering hypothesis; core pyroptosis and
    IL1/IL18 routes are prior-arted or safety-blocked.

## Decision

Close the C15ORF48-proximal therapeutic branch for now.

`C15ORF48`/MOCCI remains a strong state/readout and wet-lab ordering axis, but
the tested intervention classes all fail at least one hard gate:

- direct C15 target: no modality;
- CCL20/CCR6: prior-art and passenger biology;
- `LITAF`: no modality and no MS/genetic support;
- `CASP4`: prior-art/selectivity/safety blocked;
- endogenous brakes: either no modality/actionability or no MS/C15 support.

Return to broader intervention-first search across the lipid-lysosomal
autoimmune module, but do not use expression adjacency alone. The next branch
must start from an actionable intervention class and ask whether it intersects
the module with MS replication and cross-disease breadth.

Candidate directions to test next:

  clinical chemistry but less saturated autoimmune prior art.
- Biomarker-defined treatment-response strategies if target nomination remains
  blocked.
- Orthogonal modality evidence not already exhausted: real perturbation,
  drug-response, structural selectivity, or genetics-first signals.
