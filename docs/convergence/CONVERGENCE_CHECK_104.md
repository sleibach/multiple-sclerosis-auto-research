# Convergence Check 104 - Post-Interface And Genetics-Neighbor Depletion

Timestamp: 2026-05-28 09:34 CEST

## What The Tracks Now Believe

Interface perturbation track:
- CUX1/NFKBIZ/ELR, TWEAK/Fn14, and LIFR are biologically real interface
  responses, but none is a V3 therapeutic claim.
- Main blockers are prior-art saturation, weak MS anchor, lack of selective
  modality, or lack of FDR-stable rescue.

Resolution/efferocytosis reopener track:
- FPR2/ANXA1 and CD300 family routes remain computationally weak for V3.
- FPR2 has Crohn/UC signal but no positive MS white-matter anchor.
- CD300 family signals are non-convergent: CD300E has the disease-state signal,
  while CD300A has only a weak perturbation trend.

Genetics-first track:
- Wave164 confirms many strong autoimmune mechanisms, but no direct promotable
  target after requiring MS/cross-disease genetics, cell-state support,
  reachability, and unblocked direction.
- Wave165 shows `INAVA` is a real genetics clue, but its druggable neighbors
  lose the MS/cross-autoimmune anchor.

## Agreements

- Borrowed druggability is failing: moving from genetic mechanisms to nearby
  druggable nodes tends to lose target-resolved MS evidence.
- Borrowed biology is failing: interface/cell-state markers are often real but
  not intervention points.
- The next branch should start from candidates that already have genetics and
  local cell-state recurrence in the same gene or tightly specified mechanism.

## Disagreements Or Tensions

- `INAVA`/NOD/RIPK biology is plausible for IBD, but not for the V3
  cross-autoimmune MS-centered claim.
- Interface perturbation data are mechanistically closer than bulk signatures,
  but available public perturbation datasets are still mostly non-MS and
  pathway-crowded.

## Next Forcing Question

Find candidates satisfying both:

1. target-resolved cross-autoimmune/MS genetic support, and
2. replicated local cell-state recurrence,

before considering druggability. This reverses the failed neighbor-borrowing
strategy from Wave165.
