- Expression breadth alone keeps rediscovering markers, not target claims.

  epithelial/stromal remodeling marker. The Crohn negative context and
  response-direction conflict are serious.
- `APOC1` may reflect lipid-lysosomal myeloid biology, but systemic lipid
  metabolism makes it a weak intervention route without compartment specificity.
