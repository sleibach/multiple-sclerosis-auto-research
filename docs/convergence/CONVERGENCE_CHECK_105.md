## Agreement

- The best current modality pivot can recover biologically real and druggable
  targets, but prior-art crowding is severe.
- A credible V3 candidate must pass novelty earlier, not only after expensive
  target-quality correction.
- Local target-quality tables are incomplete for compound-first branches; saved
  external ChEMBL artifacts are now allowed when provenance is explicit.

## Disagreement / Remaining Tension

- LRRK2 failing novelty does not invalidate L1000 deconvolution. It validates
  the workflow by recovering a plausible known neuroimmune kinase.
- Phenotype-first repair hits may still be biologically important but lack
  intervention handles in current public data.

## Next Forcing Question

Search for L1000 or no-label candidates that satisfy three early gates before
deeper work:

1. no direct MS/autoimmune prior-art saturation,
2. target-quality or accessible modality from external/local data,
3. at least one MS or cross-autoimmune state anchor outside a single proxy.

Candidates failing any of these should be demoted quickly rather than carried
forward as narrative possibilities.
