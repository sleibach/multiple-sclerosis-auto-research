# V54 Progression Evidence Delta Against V37

Status: artifact-traced scope update, not a new findings scorecard. V37 remains
the historical scored synthesis. V54 does not recompute relevance or novelty
and does not convert a method boundary into a biological finding.

## Headline

No V37 item becomes evidence of disability progression or a progression target.
The treatment-response scalar remains a provisional monitoring lead, not a
progression endpoint. The coupled APC architecture remains supported at its
original structural scope, but its progression transfer is not supported.
V54 reinforces the prior complement/lipid closure, narrows lysosomal/OXPHOS
morphology to exploratory post-result context, and confirms that none of nine
pre-existing states reaches a progression-specific intervention gate.

The positive durable advance is methodological: the project now has an exact
progression acquisition contract, fail-closed inventory and semantic gates, a
blinded P1/P2 pre-registration process, and calibrated binary and event-time
power routes. Those tools make future data interpretable; they are not MS
progression evidence.

## What Strengthened

1. The complement/lipid closure is more secure because targeted lesion modules
   did not transfer across orthogonal pathology contexts.
2. The druggability discipline is operational rather than rhetorical: V54 did
   not invoke AlphaFold when no candidate passed progression association and
   direction gates.
3. Multi-lineage review proved useful through falsification. Its global-family
   and donor-estimand objections survived grounding and downgraded morphology.
4. Future progression data can now be handled mechanically without accepting
   relapse, stage, morphology, or pharmacodynamics as disability progression.

## What Weakened Or Narrowed

The strongest narrowing concerns the foamy morphology state. Locally positive
lysosomal/OXPHOS coefficients do not survive the complete sequential family,
are not lesion-stratum portable, and are substantially between-donor or
unresolved. They remain descriptive pathology context only.

The coupled APC architecture itself is not demoted: V54 tested a different,
stronger claim, portability to progression stage and lesion state, and that
claim did not pass. Keeping those scopes separate avoids both false demotion and
false promotion.

## Decision For The Medical Team

Near-term clinical value remains validation of the bounded treatment-monitoring
rule. Progression impact requires new longitudinal molecular data with repeated,
confirmed disability/PIRA outcomes. The correct progression investment is the
cohort specified in `PROGRESSION_COHORT_ACQUISITION_SPEC_V54.md`, not additional
target work on the current morphology or architecture signals.
