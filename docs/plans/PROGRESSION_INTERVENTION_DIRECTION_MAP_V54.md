# V54 Progression Intervention-Direction Map Plan

Status: frozen before execution.

## Purpose

Determine whether any progression-adjacent state tested in V54 supports a
direction-matched intervention hypothesis. The map is sequential: structural
tractability is considered only after progression association, pathogenic
direction, causal-node specificity, and selective perturbation pass. This
prevents a confident structure or a robust morphology association from
rescuing a failed biological gate.

## Frozen Sequential Gates

Each candidate must pass all gates:

1. **Progression-specific association**: family-wise-controlled support in a
   longitudinal disability/conversion analysis, or consistent support across
   two orthogonal progression-relevant contexts. A single morphology cohort is
   insufficient.
2. **Pathogenic direction**: the observed high/low state is shown to worsen or
   improve a functional progression-relevant phenotype. Association with foamy
   morphology does not determine whether the state is damaging, compensatory,
   or reparative.
3. **Causal-node specificity**: a component is separable from broad immune,
   metabolic, lesion, and composition state, and causal direction is identified.
4. **Selective perturbation**: a direction-matched perturbation moves the state
   and a clinically favorable functional readout in at least two independent
   primary-human contexts, with null/multiple-testing control.
5. **Collateral guardrails**: host defense, cell viability, myelin clearance,
   remyelination, and broad immune suppression are measured as relevant and do
   not move unfavorably.
6. **Modality fit**: the required activation, restoration, inhibition, or
   partial modulation is feasible and selective. AlphaFold prediction context
   may inform this final gate but cannot substitute for gates 1-5.

Any failed or unavailable earlier gate makes the candidate `not target-ready`.
The map records what evidence would reopen the route; it does not reinterpret
absence of a perturbation as proof that intervention cannot work.

## Frozen Candidate Set

The set is fixed from pre-existing V53/V54 modules and results:

- CD44/CXCR4 receptor state;
- HLA regulation;
- IFN/APC state;
- MIF ligand state;
- lysosomal state;
- OXPHOS;
- lipid repair;
- resolution/efferocytosis proxy;
- MOCCI inflammatory switch.

No post-result gene or target is added. V53's 24 held perturbation signatures,
causal-identifiability audit, component-specificity audit, and additive-pair
test provide the perturbation and specificity context.

## Verdicts

- `PROGRESSION_DIRECTION_RESOLVED_TARGET_REVISIT`: all six gates pass.
- `CONDITIONAL_DIRECTION_REQUIRES_DATA`: progression association passes but one
  or more downstream gates remain unresolved.
- `NO_PROGRESSION_DIRECTION_RESOLVED_INTERVENTION_ROUTE`: no candidate passes
  the progression-specific first gate.

The final verdict does not alter any V52/V53 target closure or claim that no
future progression target exists.
