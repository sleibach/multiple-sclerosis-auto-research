## Reason And Boundary

The completed n<=1,500 audit found no precision-ready design, but its best
predeclared cell was near the threshold: HR 1.5, 30% events, balanced sites had
strict probability 0.740 and minimum-seed probability 0.735. This separate
follow-up locates the conditional upper-range boundary without changing or
selectively extending that completed grid.

This remains seeded synthetic method behavior, not an empirical MS effect,
event rate, or universal cohort-size requirement.

## Frozen Extension

Run the identical generator, five effect patterns, two event regimes, two site
allocations, three seeds, 400 cohorts per seed/cell, and unchanged precision
gate at total N `1,800`, `2,100`, and `3,000`. Null and one-site-only/reversed
controls are rerun at every added N. Readiness remains aggregate >=0.80, every
seed >=0.75, both false-precision controls <=0.05, and calibrated nulls.

All cells are reported. HR 1.3 is retained even if it remains below the rule;
the extension is not stopped after HR 1.5 first passes.
