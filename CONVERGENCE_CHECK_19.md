# Convergence Check 19

Timestamp: 2026-05-27 11:47 UTC

## Trigger

Wave57 produced two foundation-model-supported intervention-first reopeners
(`CXCR2`, `IL7R`). Wave58 tested them against hard therapeutic gates.

## What Tracks Now Believe

- Foundation-model track: `CXCR2` and `IL7R` can shift selected disease-cell
  embeddings toward matched control centroids in one context each. This is
  triage evidence only.
- Local cell-state track: both have cross-disease recurrence. `CXCR2` is
  positive in Crohn disease, psoriasis, and ulcerative colitis. `IL7R` is
  positive in Crohn disease, type 1 diabetes, and ulcerative colitis.
- MS track: neither has strict MS white-matter support. `CXCR2` also lacks an
  MS Open Targets genetic anchor.
- Druggability track: `CXCR2` is chemically tractable; `IL7R` has biologic
  modality precedent through anti-CD127/IL-7R programs.
- Mechanism/prior-art track: both fail specificity. `CXCR2` collapses toward
  generic neutrophil chemotaxis. `IL7R` collapses toward canonical lymphocyte
  survival. Both are crowded.

## Agreement

All tracks agree that neither `CXCR2` nor `IL7R` should be promoted from V3.

## Disagreement

The only residual disagreement is whether `IL7R` might serve as a
stratification biomarker rather than a direct target. Current evidence is not
enough: the local signal is not MS-supported and the mechanism is not
lipid-lysosomal myeloid-specific.

## Next Forcing Question

Can the module be attacked by a non-canonical intervention point that is:

1. less prior-art crowded than canonical cytokine/chemokine axes,
2. closer to lipid handling or lysosomal stress than generic trafficking,
3. externally genetic or perturbation-supported,
4. not a marker-only nuclear/transcriptional state.

## Decision

Close `CXCR2` and `IL7R` for promotion. Pivot away from canonical trafficking
and lymphocyte survival targets unless a genotype-stratified therapeutic
response dataset gives a concrete biomarker claim.
